Investment Opportunities
If a project return is higher than the market interest rate, the opportunity set expands.
Assume a person with same income of $100,000 this year and next year faces an opportunity of investing $70,000 this year but getting back $80,000 next year. Should she invest if the market interest rate is 10%?
FV’ = $100,000 + $(100,000 - 70,000)* (1+10%) + $80,000 = $213,000.
PV’ = $(100,000 + 80,000) / (1 + 10%) + $(100,000 - 70,000) = $193,636.36.
