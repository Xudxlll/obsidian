**Exercise 1.** Compute the empirical and generator-based estimators of one-year transition matrix for the following data.
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">练习 1。根据以下数据，计算一年期迁移矩阵的经验估计量与基于生成矩阵的估计量。</span></details>

(a) There are four credit classes, A, B, C, D. At the beginning, there are 20 firms in A, B, C, each.
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）共有 A、B、C、D 四个信用等级。期初 A、B、C 各有 20 家企业。</span></details>

(b) In one month, one B rated firm migrates to C, and two B rated firms migrate to A, and both firms stay there for the rest of the year.
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）第 1 个月，一家 B 评级企业迁移到 C，两家 B 评级企业迁移到 A，此后当年内保持新评级。</span></details>

(c) In three months, one A rated firm migrates to B, and stays there for the rest of the year.
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（c）第 3 个月，一家 A 评级企业迁移到 B，此后当年内保持 B 评级。</span></details>

(d) In seven months, two C rated firms migrate to B and stay there for the rest of the year. Furthermore, one C rate firm defaults.
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（d）第 7 个月，两家 C 评级企业迁移到 B，此后当年内保持 B 评级；另外一家 C 评级企业违约。</span></details>

(e) Ten months later, two C rated firms default.
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（e）第 10 个月，两家 C 评级企业违约。</span></details>

**Solution.**
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">解答。</span></details>

$$
\widehat P_{\mathrm{empirical}}=
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac{19}{20}&\frac{1}{20}&0&0\\
\frac{2}{20}&\frac{17}{20}&\frac{1}{20}&0\\
0&\frac{2}{20}&\frac{15}{20}&\frac{3}{20}\\
0&0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

We compute generator-based estimators as follows,
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">基于生成矩阵的估计量计算如下：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_A&=2\times\frac{12-1}{12}+1\times\frac{3}{12}+(20-1)\times\frac{12}{12}=\frac{253}{12}\\
N_B&=3\times\frac{1}{12}+1\times\frac{12-3}{12}+2\times\frac{12-7}{12}+(20-3)\times\frac{12}{12}=\frac{226}{12}\\
N_C&=1\times\frac{12-1}{12}+3\times\frac{7}{12}+2\times\frac{10}{12}+(20-3-2)\times\frac{12}{12}=\frac{232}{12}\\
M_{A,B}&=1,\quad M_{A,C}=M_{A,D}=0\\
M_{B,A}&=2,\quad M_{B,C}=1,\quad M_{A,D}=0\\
M_{C,A}&=0,\quad M_{C,B}=2,\quad M_{C,D}=3
\end{aligned}
$$

The estimator becomes
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">估计量为：</span></details>

## PDF p.2

$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\lambda_{A,B}&=\frac{M_{A,B}}{N_A}=\frac{12}{253}
&\widehat\lambda_{A,C}&=\widehat\lambda_{A,D}=0\\
\widehat\lambda_{B,A}&=\frac{M_{B,A}}{N_B}=\frac{24}{226}
&\widehat\lambda_{B,C}&=\frac{M_{B,C}}{N_B}=\frac{12}{226}
&\widehat\lambda_{B,D}&=0\\
\widehat\lambda_{C,B}&=\frac{M_{C,B}}{N_C}=\frac{24}{232}
&\widehat\lambda_{C,D}&=\frac{M_{C,D}}{N_C}=\frac{36}{232}
&\widehat\lambda_{C,A}&=0
\end{aligned}
$$

The estimator of the generator matrix is given by
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">生成矩阵的估计为：</span></details>

$$
\widehat\Lambda=
\begin{pmatrix}
-\frac{12}{253}&\frac{12}{253}&0&0\\
\frac{24}{226}&-\frac{36}{226}&\frac{12}{226}&0\\
0&\frac{24}{232}&-\frac{60}{232}&\frac{36}{232}\\
0&0&0&0
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Therefore the one-year transition matrix is estimated by
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，一年期迁移矩阵估计为：</span></details>

$$
\widehat P_{\mathrm{generator}}=e^{\widehat\Lambda}=
\begin{pmatrix}
0.955993091&0.0428685943&0.00108024669&0.0000580677761\\
0.0959801271&0.857247550&0.0431801720&0.00359215070\\
0.00471211056&0.0841268869&0.774310356&0.136850647\\
0&0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Exercise 2.** A company has issued 3- and 5-year bonds with a coupon payment of 4% per year payable annually. The yields on the bonds (in terms of continuous compounding) are 4.5% and 4.75%, respectively. Assume the risk-free rate is 3.5% for all maturities, and the recovery value is 40% of the par value. Also assume default can only take place halfway through each year, i.e. at year 0.5, 1.5, 2.5, etc. Compute $P_1$ and $P_2$, which are the implied default intensities per year for years 1 to 3 and for years 4 and 5, respectively.
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">练习 2。一家公司发行了 3 年期和 5 年期债券，年票息率为 4%，每年付息一次。两只债券的连续复利收益率分别为 4.5% 和 4.75%。所有期限的无风险利率均为 3.5%，回收价值为面值的 40%。违约仅发生于每年年中，即第 0.5、1.5、2.5 年等。计算 P₁ 与 P₂，分别表示第 1 至第 3 年以及第 4、5 年的年度隐含违约强度。</span></details>

**Solution** For 3 years:
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">解答：对于 3 年期债券：</span></details>

| Time (Years) | Default Intensity (per year) | Recovery Value (\$) | Risk-Free Value (\$) | Loss Given Default (\$) | Discount Factor | PV of Expected Loss (\$) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.5 | $P_1$ | 40 | 103.0463 | 63.0463 | 0.9827 | $61.9555P_1$ |
| 1.5 | $P_1$ | 40 | 102.6456 | 62.6456 | 0.9489 | $59.4444P_1$ |
| 2.5 | $P_1$ | 40 | 102.2305 | 62.2305 | 0.9162 | $57.0156P_1$ |
| | | | | | Total: | $178.4155P_1$ |

<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">表头依次为时间（年）、年度违约强度、回收价值、无风险价值、违约损失、贴现因子、预期损失现值。金额单位为美元，Total 表示合计。</span></details>

Now since the yield is 4.5% the corporate bond price is
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">公司债券收益率为 4.5%，因此价格为：</span></details>

$$
4e^{-0.045*1}+4e^{-0.045*2}+104e^{-0.045*3}=98.3462
$$

Similarly, the default-free bond price is
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">类似地，无违约债券价格为：</span></details>

$$
4e^{-0.035*1}+4e^{-0.035*2}+104e^{-0.035*1}=101.2257
$$

Setting the difference between the corporate bond and default-free bond equal to the PV of the expected loss yields
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">令公司债券与无违约债券的价格差等于预期损失现值，得到：</span></details>

$$
101.2257-98.3462=78.4155P_1,
$$

i.e. the implied default intensity (or implied one-year default probability given no default yet) is
<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">即隐含违约强度（或已知此前尚未违约时的一年期隐含违约概率）为：</span></details>

$$
P_1=3.6721\%
$$
