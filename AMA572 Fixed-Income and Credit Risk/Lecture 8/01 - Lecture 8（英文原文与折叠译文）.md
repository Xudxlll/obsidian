# AMA572 Lecture 8 <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">AMA572 第 8 讲。</span></details>

**SDE, Gaussian Processes, and Girsanov Theorem.** <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">随机微分方程（SDE）、高斯过程（Gaussian Processes）与 Girsanov 定理。</span></details>

### 1 Stochastic Differential Equations <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">1 随机微分方程。</span></details>

As discussed before, many models in finance rely on the stochastic integral equation <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">如前所述，金融中的许多模型都依赖于随机积分方程（stochastic integral equation）：</span></details>

$$
X_t = X_0 + \int_0^t b\left(s, X_s\right)ds + \int_0^t \sigma\left(s, X_s\right)dB_s,
$$

or equivalently, in a form of the stochastic differential equation <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">或者等价地，写成随机微分方程的形式：</span></details>

$$
dX_t = b\left(t, X_t\right)dt + \sigma\left(t, X_t\right)dB_t.
$$

In this section, we study two issues: (a) whether the solution exists and, if so, when it is unique; (b) how to solve the equation, at least for some interesting special cases. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">在本节中，我们研究两个问题：(a) 解是否存在，以及若存在何时唯一；(b) 如何求解该方程，至少对若干有趣的特殊情形给出解法。</span></details>

#### 1.1 Strong and Weak Solutions <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">1.1 强解与弱解（Strong and Weak Solutions）。</span></details>

First, we have to assign a proper meaning to the solution(s) of a stochastic differential equation <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">首先，我们必须为随机微分方程的解赋予恰当的含义：</span></details>

$$
dX_t = b\left(t, X_t\right)dt + \sigma\left(t, X_t\right)dB_t, \tag{1}
$$

or written component-wise as <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">或按分量写为：</span></details>

$$
dX_t^{(i)} = b_i\left(t, X_t\right)dt + \sum_{j=1}^m \sigma_{ij}\left(t, X_t\right)dB_t^{(j)}, \quad i = 1, \dots, n,
$$

where $B_t = \left(B_t^{(1)}, \dots, B_t^{(m)}\right)$ is a standard $m$ dimensional Brownian motion. Let $\mathcal{F}_t$ be the information[^fn1] generated by the $B_t$ and an initial $n$-dimensional random vector $\xi$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其中 B_t = (B_t^(1), …, B_t^(m)) 是标准 m 维布朗运动。设 ℱ_t 是由 B_t 与初始 n 维随机向量 ξ 生成的信息（见脚注 1）。</span></details>

Of course, the differential form (1) is a short notation that the integral equation <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">当然，微分形式 (1) 只是以下积分方程的简记：</span></details>

$$
X_t = X_0 + \int_0^t b\left(s, X_s\right)ds + \int_0^t \sigma\left(s, X_s\right)dB_s.
$$

[^fn1]: Rigorously $\mathcal{F}_t$ has to be defined as the filtration $\sigma\left(\mathcal{G}_t \cup \mathcal{N}\right)$, where $\mathcal{G}_t$ is the filtration generated by $\xi$ and $B$, $\mathcal{G}_t = \sigma\left(\xi, W_s, 0 \le s \le t\right)$, and the collection of null sets $\mathcal{N}$ is defined to be $\mathcal{N} = \{N\colon \text{there exists a set } G \in \mathcal{G}_\infty, \text{ with } N \subseteq G \text{ and } \mathsf{P}(G) = 0.$（原 PDF 脚注中 $\mathcal{N}$ 集合末尾缺右花括号 `}`，且此处将布朗运动记作 $W_s$，照录。） <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">脚注 1：严格而言，ℱ_t 必须定义为信息流（filtration）σ(𝒢_t ∪ 𝒩)，其中 𝒢_t 是由 ξ 和 B 生成的信息流，𝒢_t = σ(ξ, W_s, 0 ≤ s ≤ t)，而零测集族 𝒩 定义为 𝒩 = {N：存在集合 G ∈ 𝒢_∞，使得 N ⊆ G 且 P(G) = 0}。</span></details>

There are at least two ways to assign proper meanings to the above stochastic differential equations. First, we may want to find a solution that is adapted to the Brownian sample path $B_t$, or is adapted to the history of the Brownian motion $\{\mathcal{F}_t\}$ This is called a *strong solution*. In other words, we can only construct $X_t$ based on the observation of $B_t$. The second interpretation, which is called a *weak solution*, allows one to construct $X_t$ and $B_t$ simultaneously. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">至少有两种方式可以为上述随机微分方程赋予恰当含义。第一种是寻找适应于布朗运动样本路径 B_t、或适应于布朗运动历史 {ℱ_t} 的解，这称为强解（strong solution）。换言之，我们只能依据对 B_t 的观测来构造 X_t。第二种解释称为弱解（weak solution），它允许同时构造 X_t 与 B_t。（注：原文 {ℱ_t} 后缺句号，照录。）</span></details>

In finance, most of the time we are interested in strong solutions. For example, when we study the replication of an option payoff with the underlying asset following a stochastic differential equation, we talk about strong solutions. There are cases in Monte Carlo simulation where one can get fast algorithms by doing stochastic expansions with respect to weak solutions by considering weak solutions (as one is only interested in computing expectations), instead of doing expansions with respect to strong solutions; see the book by Platen (1999). For our modeling purpose, we will focus on strong solutions. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">在金融学中，绝大多数时候我们关注的是强解。例如，当标的资产服从随机微分方程、而我们研究期权收益的复制（replication）时，讨论的就是强解。在蒙特卡洛模拟（Monte Carlo simulation）的某些情形下，由于人们只关心计算期望，通过对弱解做随机展开（而非对强解做展开）可以获得更快的算法，参见 Platen (1999) 的著作。出于我们的建模目的，本文将聚焦于强解。</span></details>

#### 1.2 Lipschitz and Linear Growth Conditions <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">1.2 Lipschitz 条件与线性增长条件（Lipschitz and Linear Growth Conditions）。</span></details>

To study the existence and uniqueness of the strong solution of a stochastic differential equations, we need to discuss some regularity conditions on the drift and volatility coefficients $b\left(t, x\right)$ and $\sigma\left(t, x\right)$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">为研究随机微分方程强解的存在性与唯一性，我们需要讨论漂移系数 b(t, x) 与波动率系数 σ(t, x) 的若干正则性条件（regularity conditions）。</span></details>

The coefficients $b\left(t, x\right)$ and $\sigma\left(t, x\right)$ are said to satisfy the Lipschitz condition if for every $0 \le t < \infty$ and every $x$ and $y$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">称系数 b(t, x) 与 σ(t, x) 满足 Lipschitz 条件，如果对任意 0 ≤ t &lt; ∞ 以及任意 x 与 y，有：</span></details>

$$
\left|b\left(t, x\right) - b\left(t, y\right)\right| + \left|\sigma\left(t, x\right) - \sigma\left(t, y\right)\right| \le C\left|x - y\right|,
$$

and are said to satisfy the linear growth condition if for every $0 \le t < \infty$ and every $x$ <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">称其满足线性增长条件，如果对任意 0 ≤ t &lt; ∞ 以及任意 x，有：</span></details>

$$
\left|b\left(t, x\right)\right|^2 + \left|\sigma\left(t, x\right)\right|^2 \le D\left(1 + \left|x\right|^2\right),
$$

where $C$ and $D$ are constants. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其中 C 与 D 为常数。</span></details>

An important result in stochastic differential equations is that if the coefficients $b\left(t, x\right)$ and $\sigma\left(t, x\right)$ satisfy the Lipschitz condition and the linear growth condition, then the strong solution of (1) exists and is unique, in the sense that if $\tilde{X}_t$ is another solution then <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">随机微分方程中的一个重要结论是：若系数 b(t, x) 与 σ(t, x) 满足 Lipschitz 条件和线性增长条件，则方程 (1) 的强解存在且唯一，其含义是：若 X̃_t 是另一个解，则</span></details>

$$
\mathsf{P}\left(X_t = \tilde{X}_t, \text{ for all } 0 \le t < \infty\right) = 1.
$$

The proof of this can be found in Karatzas and Shreve (1991) or Oksendal (1995). <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">该结论的证明可参见 Karatzas and Shreve (1991) 或 Øksendal (1995)。</span></details>

### 2 Two Counterexamples <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">2 两个反例。</span></details>

However, some examples in finance will not satisfy the Lipschitz conditions. For example, consider the following stochastic differential equation <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">然而，金融中的一些例子并不满足 Lipschitz 条件。例如，考虑如下随机微分方程：</span></details>

$$
dX_t = \mu X_t dt + \sigma (X_t)^\gamma dB_t, \quad 0 < \gamma < 1.
$$

which is a CEV process (Cox and Ross, 1976). The motivation of introducing this process is to model implied volatility smile (as we will discuss later), as the volatility in this model tends to decrease when the share price increases. For this stochastic differential equation, the Lipschitz condition reduces to <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">这是一个 CEV 过程（常方差弹性过程，Cox and Ross, 1976）。引入该过程的动机是刻画隐含波动率微笑（implied volatility smile，后文将讨论），因为在此模型中股价上升时波动率趋于下降。对于该随机微分方程，Lipschitz 条件简化为：</span></details>

$$
\left|\mu x - \mu y\right| + \left|\sigma x^\gamma - \sigma y^\gamma\right| = \left|\mu\right| \cdot \left|x - y\right| + \sigma \left|x^\gamma - y^\gamma\right| \le C\left|x - y\right|,
$$

which implies that <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">这意味着：</span></details>

$$
\frac{\left|x^\gamma - y^\gamma\right|}{\left|x - y\right|} \le C' < \infty,
$$

for some fixed constant $C' < \infty$. However, this is violated at $x = 0$ when $0 < \gamma < 1$, because <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对某个固定常数 C' &lt; ∞ 成立。然而，当 0 &lt; γ &lt; 1 时，该条件在 x = 0 处被破坏，因为：</span></details>

$$
\lim_{y \to x} \frac{\left|x^\gamma - y^\gamma\right|}{\left|x - y\right|} = \frac{dx^\gamma}{dx} = \gamma x^{\gamma-1} = \frac{\gamma}{x^{1-\gamma}} \to \infty, \quad x \to 0, \quad 0 < \gamma < 1,
$$

and we cannot find a finite constant $C' < \infty$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此无法找到有限常数 C' &lt; ∞。</span></details>

As another example, consider the Cox-Ingersoll-Ross (1985) interest rate model: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">再举一例，考虑 Cox–Ingersoll–Ross (1985) 利率模型：</span></details>

$$
dX_t = -\alpha\left(X_t - C\right)dt + \sigma\sqrt{X_t}dB_t, \quad \alpha > 0,
$$

where $X_t$ represents the spot interest rate. In this case the Lipschitz condition is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其中 X_t 表示即期利率（spot interest rate）。此时 Lipschitz 条件为：</span></details>

$$
\left|-\alpha(x - C) + \alpha(y - C)\right| + \left|\sigma\sqrt{x} - \sigma\sqrt{y}\right| = \alpha\left|x - y\right| + \sigma\left|\sqrt{x} - \sqrt{y}\right| \le C\left|x - y\right|,
$$

which is again violated at $x = 0$ for a similar reason. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">出于类似原因，该条件同样在 x = 0 处失效。</span></details>

The examples here have some implications in finance. For example, there are many solutions to the Cox-Ingersoll-Ross model due to the fact that the Lipschitz condition breaks down at $x = 0$, unless one specifies some proper condition condition. (This is a common mistake frequently made in finance.) For example, if we specify that the boundary point $0$ is a full reflecting boundary, then we can show the solution to the Cox-Ingersoll-Ross model is unique, and the solution has a marginal non-central chi-square distribution. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">这里的例子在金融学中有重要含义。例如，由于 Lipschitz 条件在 x = 0 处失效，除非指定恰当的边界条件，否则 Cox–Ingersoll–Ross 模型存在多个解。（这是金融学中常犯的一个错误。）例如，若规定边界点 0 为完全反射边界（full reflecting boundary），则可证明 Cox–Ingersoll–Ross 模型的解唯一，且其边际分布为非中心卡方分布（non-central chi-square distribution）。（注：原文 "condition condition" 疑似 "boundary condition" 笔误，照录。）</span></details>

To rigorously analyze these stochastic differential equations violating the Lipschitz condition or linear growth condition, one has to study the theory of diffusion processes with proper boundary conditions. An excellent introduction to the subject is Karlin and Taylor (1988, Vol. 2), in which there is a long chapter devoted to diffusion processes and boundary conditions. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">要严格分析这些不满足 Lipschitz 条件或线性增长条件的随机微分方程，就必须研究带恰当边界条件的扩散过程（diffusion processes）理论。该领域的一部极佳入门著作是 Karlin and Taylor (1988, 第 2 卷)，其中有一整长章专门讨论扩散过程与边界条件。</span></details>

### 3 Some Immediate Applications in Finance <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">3 金融中的若干直接应用。</span></details>

#### 3.1 Geometric Brownian <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">3.1 几何布朗运动（Geometric Brownian）。</span></details>

First we use stochastic differential equations to analyze stock prices. Consider an one-dimensional stochastic differential equation <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">首先我们用随机微分方程分析股票价格。考虑如下一维随机微分方程：</span></details>

$$
dX_t = \mu X_t dt + \sigma X_t dB_t, \quad X_0 = x_0
$$

with $\mu, x_0 \in (-\infty, \infty)$ and $\sigma > 0$ being constants. As we have discussed before, this equation can be used to model the stock price with mean return $\mu$, and volatility $\sigma$. In the notation of (1), $b\left(t, x\right) = \mu$, and $\sigma\left(t, x\right) = \sigma x$; therefore, the Lipschitz and linear growth conditions are satisfied. We aim at finding out the unique solution of it. To this end, note that if $\sigma = 0$, i.e. the random term disappears, then, from our knowledge of classical ordinary differential equation, we infer that the solution is $\exp\left(\mu X_t\right)$. In presence of randomness, i.e. $\sigma \ne 0$, let us still use the function $\log\left(x\right)$ to use if anything interesting happens. By Itô formula <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其中 μ、x₀ ∈ (−∞, ∞) 且 σ &gt; 0 均为常数。如前所述，该方程可用于刻画期望收益率为 μ、波动率为 σ 的股票价格。在方程 (1) 的记号下，b(t, x) = μ 且 σ(t, x) = σx（注：原文此处疑似漏写了 x，实应为 b(t, x) = μx；下文 exp(μX_t) 亦疑似 x₀ exp(μt) 笔误，且 "to use if" 疑似 "to see if" 笔误，均照录），因此满足 Lipschitz 条件和线性增长条件。我们的目标是求出它的唯一解。为此注意到，若 σ = 0（即随机项消失），根据经典常微分方程知识，可推断解具有指数形式。在存在随机性（即 σ ≠ 0）时，我们仍尝试使用函数 log(x)，看看会出现什么有趣的结果。由 Itô 公式（Itô formula）：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
d\left(\log\left(X_t\right)\right) &= \frac{1}{X_t}dX_t + \frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{1}{X_t^2}\right)(dX_t)^2 \\
&= \frac{1}{X_t}dX_t + \frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{1}{X_t^2}\right)(\mu X_t dt + \sigma X_t dB_t)^2 \\
&= \frac{1}{X_t}dX_t + \frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{1}{X_t^2}\right)\sigma^2 X_t^2 dt,
\end{aligned}
$$

where we have used the "multiplication table" from the Itô formula. Thus, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其中用到了 Itô 公式中的“乘法表”（multiplication table）。于是：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
d\left(\log\left(X_t\right)\right) &= \frac{1}{X_t}dX_t - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 dt \\
&= \frac{1}{X_t}(\mu X_t dt + \sigma X_t dB_t) - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 dt \\
&= \left(\mu - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2\right)dt + \sigma dB_t.
\end{aligned}
$$

Integrating both sides yields <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">两边积分可得：</span></details>

$$
\log\left(X_T\right) = \log\left(X_0\right) + \left(\mu - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2\right)T + \sigma B_T,
$$

whence <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">从而：</span></details>

$$
X_T = X_0 e^{\left(\mu - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2\right)T + \sigma B_T}. \tag{2}
$$

Although, the above derivation is rather heuristic (for example, what do we mean by saying $1/X_t$ before we know that $X_t \ne 0$.), the argument can be made rigorously by directly checking that (2) satisfies (1), via Itô formula. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">尽管上述推导相当具有启发性（例如，在尚不知道 X_t ≠ 0 之前，写 1/X_t 究竟是什么意思？），但通过用 Itô 公式直接检验 (2) 满足 (1)，即可使论证变得严格。</span></details>

Special as it is, this example nevertheless reveals many interesting features about finding solutions of stochastic differential equation. First, we try to see the solution of deterministic equations; then from which we guess what the right solution should be; finally, prove the solution is indeed the one we need. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">尽管这个例子比较特殊，它仍揭示了求解随机微分方程的许多有趣特征：首先考察确定性方程的解；接着据此猜测正确的解应该是什么形式；最后证明所猜的解确实就是我们需要的解。</span></details>

After obtaining the solution (2), naturally we would like to know the properties of it. By the SLLN, it can be easily seen that as $T \to \infty$, $X_T \to \infty$ if $\mu > \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2$, and $X_T \to 0$ if $\mu < \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2$. The intuition should be clear: the mean return should be larger enough to offset the risk for a long steady growth of stock price. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">得到解 (2) 之后，我们自然想了解它的性质。由强大数定律（SLLN）容易看出：当 T → ∞ 时，若 μ &gt; ½σ²，则 X_T → ∞；若 μ &lt; ½σ²，则 X_T → 0。其中的直觉很清楚：股票价格若要实现长期稳定增长，其平均收益率必须足够大，以抵消风险（波动率）带来的拖累。（注：原文 "larger enough" 照录。）</span></details>

For the mean and variance of the stock price $X_T$, we have solved this problem via some algebra related to the geometric Brownian motion in Section 2.2. However, another interesting way to compute the moments is by using the Itô formula. #### 3.2 Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Process <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">3.2 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 过程。</span></details>

Besides the geometric Brownian motion, another widely used process in finance is the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process, which is the solution of the SDE <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">除了几何布朗运动，金融中另一个广泛使用的过程是 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 过程，它是如下随机微分方程的解：</span></details>

$$

$$

where $\alpha > 0$, $\sigma > 0$, and $C \in R$ , all being constant and $X_0$ may be a random variable. It has been used in a famous paper by Vasicek (1977) to model interest-rate. The most important feature of (3) is mean reversion: the drift term in the SDE is negative if $X_t$ is above $C$, and is positive if $X_t$ is below $C$. It is indeed this specialty attracts Vasicek to use the process, since the interest rate appears to be mean reverting—if the interest rate is too high, it will drop, and vice versa. We will study in detail the Vasicek model later. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其中 α &gt; 0、σ &gt; 0 且 C ∈ R 均为常数，X₀ 可以是随机变量。Vasicek (1977) 在一篇著名论文中用该过程刻画利率。方程 (3) 最重要的特征是均值回复（mean reversion）：当 X_t 高于 C 时，SDE 中的漂移项为负；当 X_t 低于 C 时，漂移项为正。正是这一特性吸引了 Vasicek 采用该过程，因为利率似乎具有均值回复性——利率过高时会回落，过低时会回升。我们将在后文详细研究 Vasicek 模型。</span></details>

\end{aligned}

A Gaussian process $X\left(t\right)$ is a stochastic process such that $X\left(t_1\right)$, $X\left(t_2\right)$, and $X\left(t_n\right)$ have a multivariate normal distribution, for any $0 \le t_1 \le \dots \le t_n$. #### 6.1 A New Probability and Its Related Expectation <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">6.1 新概率测度及其对应的期望。</span></details>

Suppose $Z$ is a random variable with $\mathsf{P}\left(Z \ge 0\right) = 1$ and $\mathsf{E}\left(Z\right) = 1$. Then for any set $A$ we can define <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">假设 Z 是一个满足 P(Z ≥ 0) = 1 且 E(Z) = 1 的随机变量。那么对任意集合 A，我们可以定义：</span></details>

$$
\tilde{\mathsf{P}}\left(A\right) = \mathsf{E}\left(I\{A\} \cdot Z\right),
$$

where $I(\cdot)$ is the indicator function. Then it can be shown that $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}$ is a new probability. In fact it is easy to see that <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其中 I(·) 是示性函数（indicator function）。可以证明 P̃ 是一个新的概率测度。事实上容易看出：</span></details>

$$
\begin{gathered}
\tilde{\mathsf{P}}\left(A\right) = \mathsf{E}\left(I\{A\} \cdot Z\right) \ge 0, \\
\tilde{\mathsf{P}}\left(\Omega\right) = \mathsf{E}\left(I\{\Omega\} \cdot Z\right) = \mathsf{E}\left(Z\right) = 1,
\end{gathered}
$$

and the next problem shows that $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}$ satisfies the countable additivity in the definition of probability. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">下面的命题证明 P̃ 满足概率定义中的可数可加性（countable additivity）。</span></details>

**Proposition.** For the disjoint sets $A_1, A_2, A_3, \dots.$ such that <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">命题（Proposition）。对于互不相交的集合 A₁, A₂, A₃, …，满足：</span></details>

$$
A_i \cap A_j = \emptyset, \quad B = \cup_{i=1}^\infty A_i,

we have <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">则有：</span></details>

$$
\tilde{\mathsf{P}}\left(B\right) = \sum_{i=1}^\infty \tilde{\mathsf{P}}\left(A_i\right).
$$

*Proof.* Let $B_n = \cup_{i=1}^n A_i$. First it is clear that <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">证明。设 B_n = ∪_{i=1}^n A_i。首先显然有：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
\tilde{\mathsf{P}}\left(B_n\right) &= \mathsf{E}\left[I\{B_n\} \cdot Z\right] = \mathsf{E}\left[\sum_{i=1}^n I\{A_i\} \cdot Z\right] \tag{9} \\
&= \sum_{i=1}^n \mathsf{E}\left[I\{A_i\} \cdot Z\right] = \sum_{i=1}^n \tilde{\mathsf{P}}\left(A_i\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

Secondly, since $I(B_n)$ converges to $I(B)$ in a monotonically increasing way, we have <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其次，由于 I(B_n) 单调递增地收敛于 I(B)，我们有：</span></details>

\begin{aligned}
\tilde{\mathsf{P}}(B) &= \mathsf{E}\left[I\{B\} \cdot Z\right] = \mathsf{E}\left[\lim_{n \to \infty} I\{B_n\} \cdot Z\right] = \lim_{n \to \infty} \mathsf{E}\left[I\{B_n\} \cdot Z\right] \\
&= \lim_{n \to \infty} \tilde{\mathsf{P}}(B_n) = \lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^n \tilde{\mathsf{P}}(A_i) = \sum_{i=1}^\infty \tilde{\mathsf{P}}(A_i),
\end{aligned}
$$

where the third equality follows from the monotone convergence theorem and the fifth equality follows from (9). <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其中第三个等号由单调收敛定理（monotone convergence theorem）得出，第五个等号由 (9) 得出。</span></details>

Since $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}$ is a new probability, we may wonder what the new expectation $\tilde{\mathsf{E}}$ is. #### 6.2 Equivalent Probabilities <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">6.2 等价概率测度（Equivalent Probabilities）。</span></details>

Suppose we have two probabilities $\mathsf{P}$ and $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}$. The two probabilities are said to be equivalent if $\mathsf{P}(A) = 0$ if and only if $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}(A) = 0$. An immediate corollary is that for two equivalent probabilities $\mathsf{P}$ and $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}$ we have $\mathsf{P}(A) = 1$ if and only if $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}(A) = 1$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">假设我们有两个概率测度 P 和 P̃。若 P(A) = 0 当且仅当 P̃(A) = 0，则称这两个概率测度是等价的（equivalent）。一个直接推论是：对于两个等价概率测度 P 和 P̃，P(A) = 1 当且仅当 P̃(A) = 1。</span></details>

Equivalent probabilities are important in finance as we will see that the option price can be expressed in a "risk-neutral" probability $\mathsf{P}^*$ which is equivalent to the real world probability $\mathsf{P}$. It will be shown that we can set up hedging that can replicate the option payoff with $\mathsf{P}^*$-probability one. Since $\mathsf{P}^*$ is equivalent to the real world probability $\mathsf{P}$, we also know that the hedging works also with $\mathsf{P}$-probability one, which is what we really care in the real world. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">等价概率测度在金融学中十分重要，因为我们将看到期权价格可以在一个与现实世界概率测度 P 等价的“风险中性”概率测度 P* 下表示。并且可以证明，我们能够构造对冲策略，以 P*-概率 1 复制期权收益。由于 P* 与现实世界概率测度 P 等价，我们也就能确定该对冲策略以 P-概率 1 有效，而这正是我们在现实世界中真正关心的。</span></details>

We already know that if $\mathsf{P}(Z \ge 0) = 1$ is a random variable with $\mathsf{E}\left(Z\right) = 1$, then we can introduce a probability $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}$ such that for any set $A$ <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">我们已经知道，若随机变量 Z 满足 P(Z ≥ 0) = 1 且 E(Z) = 1，则可引入概率测度 P̃，使得对任意集合 A：</span></details>

$$
\tilde{\mathsf{P}}\left(A\right) = \mathsf{E}\left[I\{A\} \cdot Z\right]
$$

If we have a stronger assumption $\mathsf{P}(Z > 0) = 1$, then two probabilities $\mathsf{P}$ and $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}$ are equivalent. To show this, first observe that if $\mathsf{P}(A) = 0$, then $I(A) = 0$ with $\mathsf{P}$-probability one. Therefore, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">如果我们有更强的假设 P(Z &gt; 0) = 1，那么两个概率测度 P 和 P̃ 是等价的。为证明这一点，首先注意到若 P(A) = 0，则 I(A) = 0 以 P-概率 1 成立。因此：</span></details>

$$
\tilde{\mathsf{P}}\left(A\right) = \mathsf{E}\left[I\{A\} \cdot Z\right] = \mathsf{E}[0 \cdot Z] = 0.
$$

Conversely, if $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}\left(A\right) = 0$, then <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">反之，若 P̃(A) = 0，则：</span></details>

$$
0 = \tilde{\mathsf{P}}(A) = \mathsf{E}\left[I\{A\} \cdot Z\right],
$$

which, in conjunction with the fact that $I\{A\} \cdot Z \ge 0$ and $\mathsf{P}(Z > 0) = 1$, leads to <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">结合 I{A} · Z ≥ 0 以及 P(Z &gt; 0) = 1，可推出：</span></details>

$$
\mathsf{P}(A) = 0.
$$

#### 6.3 Going Back from $\tilde{\mathsf{E}}$ to $\mathsf{E}$ <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">6.3 从 Ẽ 变回 E。</span></details>

We have shown how to compute $\tilde{\mathsf{E}}$ from $\mathsf{E}$. The next question is how do we transform backward, i.e. going from $\mathsf{E}$ to $\tilde{\mathsf{E}}$. assumption that $\mathsf{P}\left(Z > 0\right) = 1$ (or equivalently $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}\left(Z > 0\right) = 1$). To see this we note that when $Z > 0$ almost surely, for any random variable $X$, we can introduced a new random variable $X/Z$. #### 6.4 An Example of Normal Random Variables <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">6.4 正态随机变量的例子。</span></details>

Suppose $X$ is a standard normal random variable with mean zero and variance one under $\mathsf{P}$. We can introduce a random variable <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">假设在测度 P 下，X 是均值为 0、方差为 1 的标准正态随机变量。我们可以引入随机变量：</span></details>

Then we can easily show that <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">那么容易证明：</span></details>

Z > 0, \quad \mathsf{E}\left[Z\right] = 1. \tag{12}

We can define a new probability $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}$ such that for any set $A$ <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">我们可以定义一个新概率测度 P̃，使得对任意集合 A：</span></details>

Then under $\mathsf{P}$, $Y$ has a distribution of $N(\theta, 1)$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">则在 P 下，Y 服从分布 N(θ, 1)。</span></details>

### 7 Generalization to Stochastic Processes via Bayes Formula <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">7 借助贝叶斯公式推广至随机过程。</span></details>

Now we shall study the change of measure for a whole process rather than just a random variable. To do this, as before, we first introduce a random variable (Radon-Nikodym derivative) $Z$ such that <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">现在我们研究整个随机过程（而不仅仅是单个随机变量）的测度变换。为此，与前面一样，首先引入一个随机变量（Radon–Nikodym 导数）Z，满足：</span></details>

$$
Z > 0, \quad \mathsf{E}\left[Z\right] = 1.
$$

Then we define a Radon-Nikodym derivative process <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">然后定义一个 Radon–Nikodym 导数过程：</span></details>

$$
Z(t) = \mathsf{E}\left[Z \mid \mathcal{F}_t\right], \quad 0 \le t \le T.
$$

It is easy to see that $Z(t)$ is a martingale with respect to $\mathsf{P}$, as <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">容易看出 Z(t) 是关于 P 的鞅，因为：</span></details>

$$
\mathsf{E}\left[Z(t) \mid \mathcal{F}_s\right] = \mathsf{E}\left[\mathsf{E}\left[Z \mid \mathcal{F}_t\right] \mid \mathcal{F}_s\right] = \mathsf{E}\left[Z \mid \mathcal{F}_s\right] = Z(s), \quad 0 \le s \le t \le T.
$$

Bayes Formula says that for any $Y \in \mathcal{F}_t$ <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">贝叶斯公式（Bayes Formula）指出，对任意 Y ∈ ℱ_t：</span></details>

$$
\tilde{\mathsf{E}}\left[Y \mid \mathcal{F}_s\right] = \frac{1}{Z(s)}\mathsf{E}\left[Y \cdot Z(t) \mid \mathcal{F}_s\right] = \mathsf{E}\left[\left.Y \cdot \frac{Z(t)}{Z(s)} \right| \mathcal{F}_s\right], \quad Y \in \mathcal{F}_t, \quad 0 \le s \le t \le T. \tag{13}
$$

### 8 Cameron and Martin Formula <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">8 Cameron and Martin 公式。</span></details>

Now we can apply the general theory of change of probabilities to a special case of Brownian motion. For any real number $\mu$, and $T > 0$, we can define a new probability $\mathsf{P}^\mu\left(\cdot\right)$ on $\mathcal{F}_T$ by <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">现在我们可以将一般性的概率测度变换理论应用于布朗运动这一特殊情形。对任意实数 μ 及 T &gt; 0，可在 ℱ_T 上定义新概率测度 P^μ(·) 为：</span></details>

$$
\mathsf{P}^\mu\left(A\right) := \mathsf{E}\left[I\{A\} \cdot Z_T\right], \quad A \in \mathcal{F}_T, \tag{14}
$$

where we have chosen a special Radon-Nikodym derivative process <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其中我们选取了一个特殊的 Radon–Nikodym 导数过程：</span></details>

$$
Z_t := \exp(\mu W_t - \mu^2 t/2).
$$

It is easy to check that $\mathsf{P}^\mu\left(A\right)$ is indeed a probability measure, because, as we have seen before that $Z_t$ is a martingale and <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">容易验证 P^μ(A) 确实是一个概率测度，因为如前所述 Z_t 是鞅，且：</span></details>

$$
\mathsf{E}\left[Z_T \mid \mathcal{F}_t\right] = Z_t, \quad \mathsf{E}\left[Z_T\right] = 1.
$$

Furthermore, it is also not difficult to see that two probability measures $\mathsf{P}^\mu\left(\cdot\right)$ and $\mathsf{P}\left(\cdot\right)$ are equivalent when restricted on $\mathcal{F}_T$ by using $Z_T$ because <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">此外，也不难看出通过 Z_T 定义的两个概率测度 P^μ(·) 与 P(·) 限制在 ℱ_T 上时是等价的，因为：</span></details>

$$
Z_T > 0.
$$

Practically, it means that when a event with non zero probability under one measure should not have zero probability under the other measure. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">从实际意义上讲，这意味着在一个测度下具有非零概率的事件，在另一个测度下的概率也不应为零。（注：原文 "a event" 照录。）</span></details>

Suppose that, under probability $\mathsf{P}$, $W_t$ is a standard Brownian motion. Then the Cameron and Martin (1944) formula states that, under the new probability measure $\mathsf{P}^\mu\left(\cdot\right)$, $\tilde{W}_t = W_t - \mu t$ is a standard Brownian motion, and therefore, $W_t = \tilde{W}_t + \mu t$ is a Brownian motion with drift $\mu$ and variance one. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">假设在概率测度 P 下，W_t 是标准布朗运动。那么 Cameron and Martin (1944) 公式指出：在新概率测度 P^μ(·) 下，W̃_t = W_t − μt 是标准布朗运动，从而 W_t = W̃_t + μt 是漂移为 μ、方差为 1 的布朗运动。</span></details>

Since the proof is relatively simple and reveals some interesting techniques, such as the Bayes formula, we shall present the proof. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">由于证明相对简单，且能展示一些有趣技巧（如贝叶斯公式），我们将给出该证明。</span></details>

To show that $\tilde{W}_t$ is a standard Brownian motion under $\mathsf{P}^\mu\left(\cdot\right)$,we need to check the definition of the standard Brownian motion. Clearly, $\tilde{W}_t = 0$, and because of the equivalence between two probability measures, $\tilde{W}_t$ also has a continuous sample path, almost surely with respect to $\mathsf{P}^\mu\left(\cdot\right)$. Therefore, we only need to show that for any increasing sequence $t_i$, $1 \le i \le m$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">要证明 W̃_t 在 P^μ(·) 下是标准布朗运动，我们需要检验标准布朗运动的定义。显然 W̃₀ = 0（注：原文误写为 "W̃_t = 0"，且逗号后缺空格，照录），并且由于两个概率测度等价，W̃_t 关于 P^μ(·) 几乎必然也具有连续样本路径。因此，我们只需证明：对任意递增序列 t_i（1 ≤ i ≤ m），有：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{P}^\mu\left(\tilde{W}_{t_1} \le x_1, \tilde{W}_{t_2} - \tilde{W}_{t_1} \le x_2, \dots, \tilde{W}_{t_m} - \tilde{W}_{t_{m-1}} \le x_m\right) \\

$$

$$
\mathsf{E}\left[\left.I\left\{\tilde{W}_{t_m} - \tilde{W}_{t_{m-1}} \le x_m\right\} \cdot \frac{Z_{t_m}}{Z_{t_{m-1}}} \right| \mathcal{F}_{m-1}\right] = \Phi\left(\frac{x_m}{\sqrt{t_m - t_{m-1}}}\right). \tag{16}
$$

Therefore, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此：</span></details>

$$
\mathsf{P}^\mu\left[\tilde{W}_{t_m} - \tilde{W}_{t_{m-1}} \le x_m \mid \mathcal{F}_{m-1}\right] = \Phi\left(\frac{x_m}{\sqrt{t_m - t_{m-1}}}\right),
$$

and <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">从而：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{P}^\mu\left(\tilde{W}_{t_1} \le x_1, \tilde{W}_{t_2} - \tilde{W}_{t_1} \le x_2, \dots, \tilde{W}_{t_m} - \tilde{W}_{t_{m-1}} \le x_m\right) \\
=\ &\mathsf{E}^\mu\left[I\left\{\tilde{W}_{t_1} \le x_1, \dots, \tilde{W}_{t_{m-1}} - \tilde{W}_{t_{m-2}} \le x_{m-1}\right\} \cdot \right. \\
&\left.\cdot \mathsf{P}^\mu\left[\tilde{W}_{t_m} - \tilde{W}_{t_{m-1}} \le x_m \mid \mathcal{F}_{m-1}\right]\right] \\
=\ &\Phi\left(x_m/\sqrt{t_m - t_{m-1}}\right)\mathsf{E}^\mu\left\{I\left\{\tilde{W}_{t_1} \le x_1, \dots, \tilde{W}_{t_{m-1}} - \tilde{W}_{t_{m-2}} \le x_{m-1}\right\}\right\} \\
=\ &\Phi\left(x_m/\sqrt{t_m - t_{m-1}}\right)\mathsf{P}^\mu\left\{\tilde{W}_{t_1} \le x_1, \dots, \tilde{W}_{t_{m-1}} - \tilde{W}_{t_{m-2}} \le x_{m-1}\right\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

### 9 Girsanov Theorem <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">9 Girsanov 定理。</span></details>

Let $W(t)$ be a standard $d$-dimensional Brownian motion under $\mathsf{P}$. For a $d$-dimensional process $\theta(s) \in \mathcal{F}_s$ consider the process <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">设 W(t) 是在 P 下的标准 d 维布朗运动。对于 d 维适应过程 θ(s) ∈ ℱ_s，考虑过程：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
Z(t) :&= \exp\left\{-\int_0^t \theta^*(s)dW(s) - \frac{1}{2}\int_0^t \left\|\theta(s)\right\|^2 ds\right\} \\
&= \exp\left\{-\sum_{i=1}^d \int_0^t \theta^{(i)}(s)dW^{(i)}(s) - \frac{1}{2}\int_0^t \left\|\theta(s)\right\|^2 ds\right\}
\end{aligned}
$$

Under the assumption $Z(t)$ is a martingale with $\mathsf{E}[Z(t)] = 1$, Girsanov theorem implies that <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">在假设 Z(t) 是满足 E[Z(t)] = 1 的鞅的条件下，Girsanov 定理推出：</span></details>

$$
\tilde{W}^{(i)}(t) := W^{(i)}(t) + \int_0^t \theta^{(i)}(s)ds, \quad 0 \le t \le T, \quad 1 \le i \le d
$$

is a standard $d$-dimensional Brownian motion under $\tilde{\mathsf{P}}$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">在测度 P̃ 下是一个标准 d 维布朗运动。</span></details>

Note that it is easy to show by using Itô's formula that $Z(t)$ satisfies a stochastic differential equation <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">注意，利用 Itô 公式很容易证明 Z(t) 满足如下随机微分方程：</span></details>

$$
Z(t) = 1 - \sum_{i=1}^d \int_0^t Z(s)\theta^{(i)}(s)dW^{(i)}(s),
$$

or <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">或者：</span></details>

$$
dZ(t) = -Z(t)\sum_{i=1}^d \theta^{(i)}(t)dW^{(i)}(t), \quad Z(0) = 1.
$$

Thus, by the definition of stochastic integral, $Z(t)$ is a local martingale with $Z(0) = 1$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，由随机积分的定义，Z(t) 是一个满足 Z(0) = 1 的局部鞅（local martingale）。</span></details>
