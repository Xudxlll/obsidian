### 3.2 印刷解答还原：Part (b) 求均值 $\mathbb{E}[r(t)]$（PDF pp.14–15，据图 233–239）

*(part (a) 末行，图 233 顶部可见)：*

$$
r(t) = e^{-H(t)}r(0) + \int_0^t e^{H(u)-H(t)}\alpha(u)du + \int_0^t e^{H(u)-H(t)}\sigma(u)dW(u).
$$

(b)

Since <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">(b) 由于：</span></details>

$$
dr(t) = (\alpha(t) - \beta(t)r(t))dt + \sigma(t)dW(t),
$$

we have <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">我们有：</span></details>

$$
d\mathbb{E}[r(t)] = (\alpha(t) - \beta(t)\mathbb{E}[r(t)])dt.
$$

First, we consider the homogeneous equation <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">首先，考虑对应的齐次方程（homogeneous equation）：</span></details>

$$
d\mathbb{E}[r(t)] = -\beta(t)\mathbb{E}[r(t)]dt.
$$

Obviously, $\mathbb{E}[r(t)] = ce^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du}$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">显然，E[r(t)] = c e^(−∫₀ᵗ β(u) du)。</span></details>

Second, we set the solution of the non-homogeneous equation as <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其次，设非齐次方程（non-homogeneous equation）的解为（常数变易法）：</span></details>

$$
\mathbb{E}[r(t)] = c(t)e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du}.
$$

Then <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">则：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
d\mathbb{E}[r(t)] &= c'(t)e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du}dt - \beta(t)c(t)e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du}dt \\
&= c'(t)e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du}dt - \beta(t)\mathbb{E}[r(t)]dt.
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, $c'(t)e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du} = \alpha(t)$, i.e., $c'(t) = \alpha(t)e^{\int_0^t \beta(u)du}$. Thus, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">从而 c'(t) e^(−∫₀ᵗ β(u) du) = α(t)，即 c'(t) = α(t) e^(∫₀ᵗ β(u) du)。因此：</span></details>

$$
c(t) = c(0) + \int_0^t \alpha(u)e^{\int_0^u \beta(v)dv}du.
$$

Since $\mathbb{E}[r(0)] = c(0)e^{-\int_0^0 \beta(u)du}$, we get $c(0) = r_0$. So, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">由于 E[r(0)] = c(0) e^(−∫₀⁰ β(u) du)，可得 c(0) = r₀。于是：</span></details>

$$
c(t) = r_0 + \int_0^t \alpha(u)e^{\int_0^u \beta(v)dv}du.
$$

*(接第 15 页顶部，右侧 Marks 栏：`5`)*

Therefore, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此（右侧分值栏：5 分）：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}[r(t)] &= \left(r_0 + \int_0^t \alpha(u)e^{\int_0^u \beta(v)dv}du\right) e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du} \\
&= r_0 e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du} + \int_0^t \alpha(u)e^{-\int_u^t \beta(v)dv}du.
\end{aligned}
$$

- **Part (b) 课堂手写推导要点汇总（图 233–239）**：
  1. **直接取期望（图 233）**：对 SDE 两端加 $\mathsf{E}[\cdot]$，圈出 $\sigma(t)dW(t)$ 并标注 `= 0`，得到 $d\mathbb{E}[r(t)] = (\alpha(t) - \beta(t)\mathbb{E}[r(t)])dt$。
  2. **换记号 $y(t) = \mathsf{E}[r(t)]$ 与常数变易法（图 234–237）**：
     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     &y(t) = \mathsf{E}[r(t)], \quad dy(t) = (\alpha(t) - \beta(t)y(t))dt \\
     &\text{齐次方程：} dy(t) = -\beta(t)y(t)dt \implies y(t) = c\,e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du} \\
     &\text{非齐次设解：} y(t) = c(t)e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du} \\
     &dy(t) = \big(\alpha(t) \cancel{- \beta(t)y(t)}\big)dt = c'(t)e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du}dt \cancel{- \beta(t)\mathbb{E}[r(t)]}dt \\
     &\implies \alpha(t) = c'(t)e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du}
     \end{aligned}
     $$
  3. **初值确定（图 238）**：取 $t = 0$，老师手写强调 $r_0$ 为确定常数（举例 `= 1,000,000`）：
     $$
     \mathsf{E}[r(0)] = c(0) = \mathsf{E}[r_0] = r_0
     $$
  4. **代回（图 239）**：跨页标注 $\mathbb{E}[r(t)] = c(t)e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du}$，将 $c(t)$ 代入并化简指数得最终式。

### 3.3 印刷解答还原：Part (c) 求方差 $\operatorname{Var}(r(t))$（PDF p.15，据图 241–243）

(c)

From (b), we have <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">(c) 由 (b) 可得：</span></details>

$$
d(r(t) - \mathbb{E}[r(t)]) = -\beta(t)(r(t) - \mathbb{E}[r(t)])dt + \sigma(t)dW(t).
$$

Set $y(t) := r(t) - \mathbb{E}[r(t)]$. Then <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">令 y(t) := r(t) − E[r(t)]，则：</span></details>

$$
dy(t) = -\beta(t)y(t)dt + \sigma(t)dW(t).
$$

Hence, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此：</span></details>

$$
d(y(t)^2) = 2y(t)dy(t) + dy(t)dy(t) = [-2\beta(t)y(t)^2 + \sigma(t)^2]dt + 2\sigma(t)y(t)dW(t).
$$

Thus, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">于是：</span></details>

$$
d\mathbb{E}[y(t)^2] = \left[-2\beta(t)\mathbb{E}[y(t)^2] + \sigma(t)^2\right] dt.
$$

So, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">从而（注：原文此处与下文积分号内均将 σ(u)² 误印为 σ(t)²，照录）：</span></details>

$$
\mathbb{E}[y(t)^2] = \int_0^t \sigma(t)^2 e^{-2\int_u^t \beta(v)dv} du.
$$

Therefore, *(右侧 Marks 栏：`5`)* <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此（右侧分值栏：5 分；正确积分变量应为 σ(u)²）：</span></details>

$$
\operatorname{Var}(r(t)) = \mathbb{E}[y(t)^2] = \int_0^t \sigma(t)^2 e^{-2\int_u^t \beta(v)dv} du.
$$

- **Part (c) 课堂手写推导要点汇总（图 241–243）**：
  1. **用 Itô 引理显式展开 $d(y(t)^2)$（图 241）**：老师划掉印刷的 $2y(t)dy(t)+dy(t)dy(t)$，改按标准偏导展开：
     $$
     f(t, y) = y(t)^2, \quad \frac{\partial f}{\partial t} = 0, \quad \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} = 2y, \quad \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2} = 2
     $$
     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     d(y(t)^2) &= \frac{\partial f}{\partial t}dt + \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}dy + \frac{1}{2}\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2}\sigma(t)^2 dt \\
     &= 0\,dt + 2y\,dy + \frac{1}{2}\times 2 \times \sigma(t)^2 dt \\
     &= 2y(t)\big(-\beta(t)y(t)dt + \sigma(t)dW(t)\big) + \sigma^2(t)dt \\
     &= \big(-2\beta(t)y(t)^2 + \sigma^2(t)\big)dt + 2\sigma(t)y(t)dW(t)
     \end{aligned}
     $$
  2. **取期望与齐次初值项为何消失（图 242–243）**：
     - 蓝斜线划去 $+2\sigma(t)y(t)dW(t)$（取期望为 $0$），得到关于 $\mathbb{E}[y(t)^2]$ 的一阶线性 ODE；
     - 图 242 在 $\mathbb{E}[y(t)^2] = \int_0^t \dots$ 右侧画了三个红色问号 `???`，提问“为何不像 (b) 中均值那样有齐次初值项 $r_0 e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du}$？”；
     - 图 243 在 (b) 的 $r_0 e^{-\int_0^t \beta(u)du}$ 下画红三角 `Δ`，并在 (c) 的 `=` 与 `\int_0^t` 之间用红插入符补标 `0 +`（含红三角 `Δ`），点明由于初值 $r_0$ 确定，初始方差 $\mathbb{E}[y(0)^2] = \operatorname{Var}(r_0) = 0$，故齐次项为 $0$。

### 4.2 印刷解答完整还原（PDF p.17，据图 220–223）

**Question 6** *(Marks: `15`)*

Since <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">由于：</span></details>

$$
dr(t) = a(b - r(t))dt + \sigma\sqrt{r(t)}dW(t),
$$

we have <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">我们有：</span></details>

$$
d\mathbb{E}[r(t)] = a(b - \mathbb{E}[r(t)])dt.
$$

Therefore, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此（注：原 PDF 连续重复印了两行 `= (r_0 + ∫₀ᵗ ab e^(au) du) e^(−at)`，此处照录）：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}[r(t)] &= \left(r_0 + \int_0^t ab e^{\int_0^u a dv} du\right) e^{-\int_0^t a du} \\
&= \left(r_0 + \int_0^t ab e^{au} du\right) e^{-at} \\
&= \left(r_0 + \int_0^t ab e^{au} du\right) e^{-at} \\
&= r_0 e^{-at} + b(1 - e^{-at}).
\end{aligned}
$$

Next, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">接下来：</span></details>

$$
d(r(t) - \mathbb{E}[r(t)]) = -a(r(t) - \mathbb{E}[r(t)])dt + \sigma\sqrt{r(t)}dW(t).
$$

Set $y(t) := r(t) - \mathbb{E}[r(t)]$. Then <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">令 y(t) := r(t) − E[r(t)]，则：</span></details>

$$
dy(t) = -ay(t)dt + \sigma\sqrt{r(t)}dW(t).
$$

Hence, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此：</span></details>

$$
d(y(t)^2) = 2y(t)dy(t) + dy(t)dy(t) = [-2ay(t)^2 + \sigma^2 r(t)]dt + 2\sigma\sqrt{r(t)}y(t)dW(t).
$$

Thus, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">于是：</span></details>

$$
d\mathbb{E}[y(t)^2] = \left(-2a\mathbb{E}[y(t)^2] + \sigma^2 \mathbb{E}[r(t)]\right) dt.
$$

So, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">从而：</span></details>

$$
\mathbb{E}[y(t)^2] = \int_0^t \sigma^2 \mathbb{E}[r(u)] e^{-2\int_u^t a dv} du = e^{-2at}\sigma^2 \int_0^t \mathbb{E}[r(u)] e^{2au} du.
$$

Therefore, *(右侧 Marks 栏：`15`)* <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此（右侧分值栏：15 分）：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(r(t)) &= \mathbb{E}[y(t)^2] = e^{-2at}\sigma^2 \int_0^t \mathbb{E}[r(u)] e^{2au} du \\
&= e^{-2at}\sigma^2 \int_0^t \left[r_0 e^{-au} + b(1 - e^{-au})\right] e^{2au} du \\
&= e^{-2at}\sigma^2 \int_0^t \left[r_0 e^{au} + b(e^{2au} - e^{au})\right] du \\
&= e^{-2at}\frac{\sigma^2}{a}\left[r_0(e^{at} - 1) + \frac{b}{2}(e^{2at} - 1) - b(e^{at} - 1)\right] \\
&= e^{-2at}\frac{\sigma^2}{a}\left[r_0(e^{at} - 1) + \frac{b}{2}e^{2at} - be^{at} + \frac{b}{2}\right] \\
&= e^{-2at}\frac{\sigma^2}{a}\left[r_0(e^{at} - 1) + \frac{b}{2}(e^{at} - 1)^2\right] \\
&= \frac{\sigma^2}{a}\left[r_0(e^{-at} - e^{-2at}) + \frac{b}{2}(1 - e^{-at})^2\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

- **Question 6 课堂手写标注要点（图 220–223）**：
  - 图 220：紫笔把 $\mathbb{E}[r(t)] = r_0 e^{-at} + b(1 - e^{-at})$ 中的变量 $t$ 覆写为哑变量 $u$（$\mathbb{E}[r(u)] = r_0 e^{-au} + b(1 - e^{-au})$），为下方代入积分做准备；蓝笔标出中心化过程 $y(t) := r(t) - \mathbb{E}[r(t)]$ 及其 SDE。
