### 题目

#### Question 1 (20 marks): Rating transitions <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 1 题（20 分）：评级迁移。</span></details>

Given the following information: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">已知以下信息：</span></details>

(a) There are four credit classes $A,B,C,D$, where $D$ is default (absorbing). Initially, there are 24 firms in each of $A,B,C$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）有 A、B、C、D 四种信用等级，D 为违约吸收状态。最初 A、B、C 各有 24 家企业。</span></details>

(b) After one month, two $B$ firms migrate to $C$ and two $B$ firms migrate to $A$; they remain there during the rest of the year. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）一个月后，两家 B 级企业迁至 C，另两家 B 级企业迁至 A；此后至年末均保持新等级。</span></details>

(c) After three months, two $A$ firms migrate to $B$, and remain there. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（c）三个月后，两家 A 级企业迁至 B，并保持至年末。</span></details>

(d) After seven months, three $C$ firms migrate to $B$, and two $C$ firms default. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（d）七个月后，三家 C 级企业迁至 B，两家 C 级企业违约。</span></details>

(e) After ten months (measured from the start of the year), two further $C$ firms default. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（e）从年初算起十个月后，再有两家 C 级企业违约。</span></details>

Compute (1) the empirical one-year transition matrix, (2) the estimated generator $\widehat\Lambda$, and (3) the generator-based one-year transition matrix. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">计算：（1）经验一年期迁移矩阵；（2）生成矩阵估计 Λ̂；（3）基于生成矩阵的一年期迁移矩阵。</span></details>

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Detailed solution <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">详细解答。</span></details>

**Step 1: terminal transitions by initial cohort.** Initially-$A$: 22 stay $A$, two move to $B$. Initially-$B$: two move to $A$, 20 stay $B$, two move to $C$. Initially-$C$: three move to $B$, 17 stay $C$, four default. Thus, in state order $(A,B,C,D)$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 1 步：按初始队列统计年末迁移。原 A 级：22 家留在 A、2 家转至 B；原 B 级：2 家转 A、20 家留 B、2 家转 C；原 C 级：3 家转 B、17 家留 C、4 家违约。按 (A，B，C，D) 排序：</span></details>

$$
\widehat P_{\mathrm{empirical}}=\begin{pmatrix}22/24&2/24&0&0\\2/24&20/24&2/24&0\\0&3/24&17/24&4/24\\0&0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**Step 2: total exposure time in years.** Let $N_i$ be total years-at-risk spent in rating $i$ by all firms; individuals that migrate contribute exposure to multiple ratings. The time of each event is measured from the initial date: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 2 步：以年为单位统计总暴露时间。Ni 为全部企业在等级 i 中处于风险下的总年数；迁移企业对多个等级贡献暴露时间。各事件时间均从初始日计算：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_A&=22(1)+2\left(\frac3{12}\right)+2\left(\frac{11}{12}\right)=\frac{73}3=24.333333,\\
N_B&=20(1)+4\left(\frac1{12}\right)+2\left(\frac9{12}\right)+3\left(\frac5{12}\right)=\frac{277}{12}=23.083333,\\
N_C&=17(1)+5\left(\frac7{12}\right)+2\left(\frac{10}{12}\right)+2\left(\frac{11}{12}\right)=\frac{281}{12}=23.416667.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here 4 in $N_B$ represents the four original $B$-rated firms leaving after month 1; 5 in $N_C$ represents the three migrations and two defaults at month 7. The two firms arriving in $C$ at month 1 contribute 11/12 year each. **Step 3: generator estimation.** For $i\ne j$, use $\widehat\lambda_{ij}=M_{ij}/N_i$, and set $\widehat\lambda_{ii}=-\sum_{j\ne i}\widehat\lambda_{ij}$. The event counts are $M_{AB}=2$, $M_{BA}=2$, $M_{BC}=2$, $M_{CB}=3$, $M_{CD}=4$. Hence <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">NB 中的 4 表示第 1 个月后离开的四家原 B 级企业；NC 中的 5 表示第 7 个月的三次迁移和两次违约。第 1 个月进入 C 的两家企业各贡献 11／12 年。第 3 步：估计生成矩阵。对 i≠j 使用 λ̂ij＝Mij／Ni，并令 λ̂ii＝−Σj≠i λ̂ij。事件次数为 MAB＝2、MBA＝2、MBC＝2、MCB＝3、MCD＝4。因此：</span></details>

$$
\widehat\Lambda=\begin{pmatrix}-6/73&6/73&0&0\\24/277&-48/277&24/277&0\\0&36/281&-84/281&48/281\\0&0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**Step 4: matrix exponential.** For a one-year horizon $\Delta t=1$, the continuous-time Markov estimator is $e^{\widehat\Lambda\Delta t}$. Numerically, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 4 步：矩阵指数。一年期 Δt＝1 时，连续时间马尔可夫估计为 e^(Λ̂Δt)。数值上：</span></details>

$$
\widehat P_{\mathrm{generator}}\approx\begin{pmatrix}0.924282&0.072574&0.002967&0.000177\\0.076504&0.848474&0.068679&0.006344\\0.004625&0.101552&0.745910&0.147913\\0&0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**Checks.** Rows sum to one (up to rounding). $D$ is absorbing. Unlike the empirical matrix, the generator matrix produces small positive probabilities for indirect paths such as $A\to B\to C$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">检查：忽略舍入误差，各行之和为 1，D 为吸收状态。与经验矩阵不同，基于生成矩阵的结果对 A→B→C 等间接路径给出较小的正概率。</span></details>

### 中文详细讲解

**先认清数据与第一套不同。**这次是 A、B、C、D 四个状态，A、B、C 期初各 24 家；月份是 1、3、7、10 月，不能拿第一套的矩阵改一下大小就用。

**第一步：经验矩阵按期初群体统计。**初始 A 中 22 家留 A、2 家到 B；初始 B 中 2 家到 A、20 家留 B、2 家到 C；初始 C 中 3 家到 B、17 家留 C、4 家违约。每行除以 24，最后的违约行由吸收态假设设为 $(0,0,0,1)$。

**第二步：把每一组时间拆清楚。**

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_A&=22+2\frac3{12}+2\frac{11}{12}=\frac{73}{3},\\
N_B&=20+4\frac1{12}+2\frac9{12}+3\frac5{12}=\frac{277}{12},\\
N_C&=17+5\frac7{12}+2\frac{10}{12}+2\frac{11}{12}=\frac{281}{12}.
\end{aligned}
$$

例如 $N_C$：17 家全年留在 C；3 家迁去 B 和 2 家违约，共 5 家在 C 待到 7 月；另外 2 家在 C 待到 10 月；最后还加上 1 月从 B 迁入的 2 家各自的 11 个月。**迁入、迁出都要按真实在场时长累计。**10 月是从年初计时，不是 7 月之后再过十个月。

**第三步：数事件，再除以出发状态的累计时间。**非零次数为 $M_{AB}=2,M_{BA}=2,M_{BC}=2,M_{CB}=3,M_{CD}=4$。例如：

$$
\widehat\lambda_{AB}=\frac2{73/3}=\frac6{73},
\qquad
\widehat\lambda_{CD}=\frac4{281/12}=\frac{48}{281}.
$$

对角线设成负的离开强度总和，就得到原答案的 $\widehat\Lambda$。

**第四步：求矩阵指数，得到一年概率。**原答案的六位小数矩阵已独立复算一致。例如：

$$
\widehat P_{AD}(1)\approx0.000176877,
\qquad \widehat P_{CD}(1)\approx0.147912924.
$$

虽然样本中没见 A 直接违约，拟合后的连续时间模型允许 A→B→C→D 的多次路径，因此 $P_{AD}(1)$ 仍可为正。要区分：样本里“没发生”、直接强度估计为 0，并不等于未来一整年“绝对到不了”。

**最终核验：**$N_A+N_B+N_C=70.833333$ 公司年，小于 $72$，因为违约后停止累计非违约暴露时间；生成矩阵各行和为 0，概率矩阵各行和为 1。

### 题目

#### Question 2 (20 marks): Implied default parameters <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 2 题（20 分）：隐含违约参数。</span></details>

A company issues three- and five-year bonds with an annual coupon of 4.2% of par, paid annually. Their continuously compounded yields are 4.6% and 4.9%, respectively. The flat continuously compounded risk-free rate is 2.7%; recovery at default is 45% of par. Assume default can occur only halfway through a year, at 0.5, 1.5, 2.5,.... Using the course’s expected-loss approximation, calculate $P_1$ for years 1–3 and $P_2$ for years 4–5. Take par value as 100. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">企业发行三年期和五年期债券，每年付息，年票息为面值的 4.2%，连续复利收益率分别为 4.6% 和 4.9%。平坦连续复利无风险利率为 2.7%，违约回收为面值的 45%。假设只在年中 0.5、1.5、2.5、……违约。使用课程的预期损失近似，计算第 1—3 年的 P1 和第 4—5 年的 P2，取面值 100。</span></details>

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Detailed solution <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">详细解答。</span></details>

**Valuation convention.** The course’s midterm approximates default losses by multiplying the discounted loss in each year by $P_1$ or $P_2$ and summing, without compounding survival probabilities. Accordingly, $P_1,P_2$ below are piecewise annual default-probability parameters under that approximation, not exact continuously compounded hazard intensities. For a maturity-$T$ bond, the risk-free value of its remaining promised cash flows as seen at $t$ (immediately before default) is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">估值口径：课程期中题把各年的贴现损失直接乘以 P1 或 P2 后相加，不复合计算存续概率。因此下文 P1、P2 是这一近似下分段的年度违约概率参数，并非精确的连续复利风险强度。对到期日为 T 的债券，t 时刻（紧接违约前）剩余承诺现金流的无风险价值为：</span></details>

$$
F_T^{\mathrm{rf}}(t)=\sum_{k=\lfloor t\rfloor+1}^T4.2e^{-0.027(k-t)}+100e^{-0.027(T-t)}.
$$

The loss upon default is $L_T(t)=F_T^{\mathrm{rf}}(t)-45$, with time-zero present value factor $e^{-0.027t}$. **(I) Three-year bond.** Prices inferred from promised cash flows are <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">违约损失为 LT(t)＝FT^rf(t)−45，贴现至零时刻的因子为 e^(−0.027t)。（I）三年期债券：由承诺现金流得到的价格为：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
B_3^{\mathrm{rf}}&=4.2e^{-0.027}+4.2e^{-2(0.027)}+104.2e^{-3(0.027)}=104.159915,\\
B_3^{\mathrm{risky}}&=4.2e^{-0.046}+4.2e^{-2(0.046)}+104.2e^{-3(0.046)}=98.610502.
\end{aligned}
$$

| $t$ | $F_3^{\mathrm{rf}}(t)$ | Recovery | Loss $L_3(t)$ | $e^{-rt}$ | Discounted loss |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 105.5756 | 45 | 60.5756 | 0.986591 | 59.7633 |
| 1.5 | 104.2079 | 45 | 59.2079 | 0.960309 | 56.8579 |
| 2.5 | 102.8028 | 45 | 57.8028 | 0.934728 | 54.0298 |

<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">各列依次为：违约时间 t、剩余承诺支付的无风险价值、回收金额、违约损失、贴现因子、贴现损失。</span></details>


Therefore, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，</span></details>

$$
104.15991471-98.61050155=(59.76333248+56.85788509+54.02983522)P_1,
$$

$$
P_1=\frac{5.54941316}{170.65105279}=0.03251907\approx3.2519\%.
$$

**(II) Five-year bond.** Similarly, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（II）五年期债券：类似地，</span></details>

\begin{aligned}

### 题目

#### Question 3 (20 marks): Tax shield and bankruptcy costs <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 3 题（20 分）：税盾（tax shield）与破产成本。</span></details>

Consider the Black–Scholes–Merton geometric Brownian motion model <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">考虑 Black–Scholes–Merton 几何布朗运动模型：</span></details>

$$
dV(t)=rV(t)dt+\sigma V(t)dW(t),\quad V(0)=1,
$$

where $r$ and $\sigma$ are constants. Compute <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其中 r 和 σ 为常数。计算：</span></details>

$$
A(0)=E^*[e^{-rT}\kappa D\mathbf1_{\{V(T)\geq D\}}],\quad C(0)=E^*[e^{-rT}(1-\alpha)V(T)\mathbf1_{\{V(T)<D\}}].
$$

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Detailed solution <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">详细解答。</span></details>

**Step 1: solve the SDE.** By Itô’s lemma for $\log V(t)$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 1 步：求解随机微分方程（SDE）。对 log V(t) 使用伊藤引理（Itô’s lemma），</span></details>

$$
d\log V(t)=\left(r-\frac12\sigma^2\right)dt+\sigma dW(t).
$$

Therefore (taking $V(0)=1$), <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此（取 V(0)＝1），</span></details>

$$
V(T)=\exp\left\{\left(r-\frac12\sigma^2\right)T+\sigma\sqrt T Z\right\},\quad Z\sim N(0,1),
$$

and $X:=\log V(T)\sim N(\mu,s^2)$, where $\mu=(r-\sigma^2/2)T$, $s=\sigma\sqrt T$. Let $\Phi$ denote the standard normal CDF. Then <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">且 X＝log V(T)∼N(μ，s²)，其中 μ＝(r−σ²／2)T，s＝σ√T。以 Φ 表示标准正态累积分布函数，则：</span></details>

$$
P^*(V(T)\geq D)=P^*(X\geq\log D)=\Phi\left(\frac{\mu-\log D}s\right).
$$

**Step 2: tax benefit.** Directly, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 2 步：税收收益。直接得到：</span></details>

$$
A(0)=\kappa De^{-rT}\Phi\left(\frac{(r-\sigma^2/2)T-\log D}{\sigma\sqrt T}\right).
$$

**Step 3: bankruptcy cost.** Write the truncated lognormal expectation as <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 3 步：破产成本。把截断对数正态期望写为：</span></details>

$$
E^*[V(T)\mathbf1_{\{V(T)<D\}}]=\int_{-\infty}^{\log D}e^x\frac1{s\sqrt{2\pi}}\exp\left[-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2s^2}\right]dx.
$$

Complete the square, using <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">利用下式配方：</span></details>

$$
x-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2s^2}=\mu+\frac{s^2}2-\frac{[x-(\mu+s^2)]^2}{2s^2}.
$$

Consequently, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^*[V(T)\mathbf1_{\{V(T)<D\}}]&=e^{\mu+s^2/2}\Phi\left(\frac{\log D-\mu-s^2}s\right)\\
&=e^{rT}\Phi\left(\frac{\log D-(r+\sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma\sqrt T}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

Multiplying by $e^{-rT}(1-\alpha)$ gives <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">乘以 e^(−rT)(1−α)，得：</span></details>

$$
C(0)=(1-\alpha)\Phi\left(\frac{\log D-(r+\sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma\sqrt T}\right).
$$

**Check.** For $D\downarrow0$, both quantities tend to zero. The tax shield is discounted by $e^{-rT}$, while the risk-neutral asset expectation cancels that factor in $C(0)$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">检查：当 D↓0 时，两项均趋于零。税盾需乘 e^(−rT) 贴现，而 C(0) 中风险中性资产期望会抵消该贴现因子。</span></details>

### 中文详细讲解

**先辨别两个期望的差别。**税收利益 $A(0)$ 是“固定金额 $\kappa D$，但只在存活时支付”；破产成本 $C(0)$ 是“随机资产 $V_T$ 的一部分，但只在违约时损失”。前者可以直接变成固定金额乘概率，后者不能。

**第一步：用 Itô 引理确定分布。**$V_0=1$，所以 $\ln V_0=0$。对 $\ln V_t$ 求随机微分并积分，得到：

$$
\ln V_T=(r-\tfrac12\sigma^2)T+\sigma\sqrt T Z,
\qquad Z\sim N(0,1).
$$

记：

$$
d_2=\frac{-\ln D+(r-\sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma\sqrt T},
\qquad d_1=d_2+\sigma\sqrt T.
$$

则存活概率为 $\Phi(d_2)$，违约概率为 $\Phi(-d_2)$。

**第二步：税收利益是固定金额乘存活概率，再折现。**

$$
A(0)=\kappa De^{-rT}\Phi(d_2).
$$

这里的税收利益是题目给定的模型现金流；不将它当成现实税法对本金的普遍规则。

**第三步：破产成本要做截断对数正态期望。**把 $V_T$ 写成指数，并用 $Z<-d_2$ 表示违约，剩下的关键积分是 $\int_{-\infty}^{-d_2}e^{aZ}\phi(Z)\,dZ$，其中 $a=\sigma\sqrt T$。

使用配方：

$$
az-\frac{z^2}{2}=-\frac{(z-a)^2}{2}+\frac{a^2}{2}.
$$

因此平移变量后，上限从 $-d_2$ 变成 $-d_2-a=-d_1$，最后得到：

$$
E^*[V_T\mathbf1_{\{V_T<D\}}]=e^{rT}\Phi(-d_1),
$$

$$
C(0)=e^{-rT}(1-\alpha)e^{rT}\Phi(-d_1)
=(1-\alpha)\Phi(-d_1).
$$

**最终：**税收利益对应 $d_2$，破产资产期望对应 $d_1$。$C(0)$ 最终没有显式 $e^{-rT}$，是因为资产风险中性增长因子与折现相消，不是因为破产成本不用折现。

若初始资产改成一般 $V_0$，需在 $d_1,d_2$ 中恢复 $\ln(V_0/D)$，且 $C(0)=(1-\alpha)V_0\Phi(-d_1)$。本题 $V_0=1$ 才省略这个因子。$D$ 下降至 0 时，两种价值都趋于 0，是简单的边界检查。

### 题目

#### Question 4 (20 marks): Leland–Toft firm and debt values <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 4 题（20 分）：Leland–Toft 企业与债务价值。</span></details>

The firm’s asset value satisfies <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">企业资产价值满足：</span></details>

$$
\frac{dV_t}{V_t}=(r-\delta)dt+\sigma dW_t,\quad V_t=V_0\exp\{(r-\delta-\sigma^2/2)t+\sigma W_t\}.
$$

(a) If $\tau=\inf\{t\geq0:V_t\leq V_B\}$ and <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）若 τ＝inf{t≥0：Vt≤VB}，且：</span></details>

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Detailed solution <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">详细解答。</span></details>

Set $h_a:=\sqrt{\xi^2+2a/\sigma^2}+\xi$. Because $\log(V_t/V)=(r-\delta-\sigma^2/2)t+\sigma W_t$ and the barrier is below $V$, Lecture 3’s downward first-passage Laplace transform gives <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">设 ha＝√(ξ²＋2a／σ²)＋ξ。由于 log(Vt／V)＝(r−δ−σ²／2)t＋σWt，且边界低于 V，第 3 讲的向下首次穿越拉普拉斯变换给出：</span></details>

$$
H(a):=E[e^{-a\tau}\mathbf1_{\{\tau<\infty\}}]=\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^{h_a}\quad(a>0).
$$

**(a) Firm value.** Integrating pathwise and taking expectations, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）企业价值。逐路径积分并取期望，</span></details>

$$
E\left[\int_0^\tau e^{-rt}dt\right]=\frac{1-E[e^{-r\tau}]}r=\frac{1-H(r)}r.
$$

Thus <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
v(V;V_B)&=V+\frac{\kappa\rho P}r(1-H(r))-(1-\alpha)V_BH(r)\\
&=V+\frac{\kappa\rho P}r-\left[\frac{\kappa\rho P}r+(1-\alpha)V_B\right]\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^{h_r}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**(b) Debt value.** With unit face value and continuously paid coupon $\rho$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）债务价值。对于面值 1、连续付息率 ρ 的债券，</span></details>

$$
B(V;V_B,T)=E\left[e^{-rT}\mathbf1_{\{\tau>T\}}+ce^{-rT}\mathbf1_{\{\tau\leq T\}}+\int_0^{\tau\wedge T}\rho e^{-rs}ds\right].
$$

Interchanging nonnegative integrations in the aggregate bond valuation gives three components (write $h=H(m+r)$): <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">在债券整体估值中交换非负积分，得到三部分（记 h＝H(m＋r)）：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{\mathrm{principal}}&=\int_0^\infty me^{-(m+r)T}P(\tau>T)dT=\frac m{m+r}(1-h),\\
I_{\mathrm{coupon}}&=\rho\int_0^\infty me^{-mT}E\left[\int_0^{\tau\wedge T}e^{-rs}ds\right]dT=\frac\rho{m+r}(1-h),\\
I_{\mathrm{recovery}}&=c\int_0^\infty me^{-(m+r)T}P(\tau\leq T)dT=\frac{mc}{m+r}h.
\end{aligned}
$$

For instance, the first equality uses $\int_0^\tau me^{-(m+r)T}dT=m(1-e^{-(m+r)\tau})/(m+r)$. For the coupon component, swapping the $s$ and $T$ integrals yields $\int_s^\infty me^{-mT}dT=e^{-ms}$. Combining all three yields <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">例如，第一个等式使用 ∫₀^τ me^(−(m＋r)T)dT＝m(1−e^(−(m＋r)τ))／(m＋r)。票息项交换 s、T 积分后有 ∫s^∞ me^(−mT)dT＝e^(−ms)。合并三项，得到：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
D(V;V_B)&=P\left[\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}(1-h)+\frac{mc}{m+r}h\right]\\
&=P\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}-\left[P\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}-\alpha V_B\right]h.
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，</span></details>

$$
D(V;V_B)=P\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}-\left[P\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}-\alpha V_B\right]\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^{h_{m+r}}.
$$

Expanding its coefficient gives exactly the more elaborate expression in the supplied examination: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">展开系数后，恰好得到所提供试卷中较复杂的表达式：</span></details>

$$
D=P\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}-P\left[\frac\rho r+\left(1-\frac\rho r-\frac{m+r}m\frac{\alpha V_B}P\right)\frac m{m+r}\right]\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^{h_{m+r}}.
$$

### 中文详细讲解

**先把两小问放到同一张图里。**（a）给整家企业估值，依靠税盾和破产损失；（b）把不同到期日的单债价格加起来，得到总债务市场价值。两者之差才是股权价值。

**共同准备：把违约折现期望写成一个函数。**令：

$$
h_a=\sqrt{\xi^2+2a/\sigma^2}+\xi,
\qquad H(a)=E^*[e^{-a\tau}]=(V_B/V)^{h_a}.
$$

这是 Lecture 3 下穿公式的应用，$a>0$；若永不违约则指数项取零。$H(a)$ 已经在一个数里包含随机违约时间及折现。

**（a）先把随机终点的税盾积分算出来。**每条路径上都有 $\int_0^\tau e^{-rt}dt=(1-e^{-r\tau})/r$，所以：

$$
v=V+\frac{\kappa\rho P}{r}[1-H(r)]-(1-\alpha)V_BH(r).
$$

把 $H(r)$ 换成幂函数并整理，即得：

$$
v(V;V_B)=V+\frac{\kappa\rho P}{r}
-\left[\frac{\kappa\rho P}{r}+(1-\alpha)V_B\right](V_B/V)^{h_r}.
$$

$\kappa\rho P/r$ 看似永续税盾现值，但紧随其后的减项扣除了违约以后拿不到的税盾，不能只看前两项就说税盾永远持续。

**（b）总债务分成本金、票息、回收三部分。**这里的积分变量 $T$ 是不同债券的剩余期限；票息积分里的 $t$ 是支付时点，两个时间不能混作一个。

令 $h=H(m+r)$。把每一部分对期限密度 $me^{-mT}$ 加权并取期望后，单位总面值对应的结果分别是：

$$
I_{\mathrm{principal}}=\frac{m}{m+r}(1-h),
\qquad I_{\mathrm{coupon}}=\frac{\rho}{m+r}(1-h),
\qquad I_{\mathrm{recovery}}=\frac{mc}{m+r}h.
$$

为什么本金与票息都出现 $1-h$？它们只在尚未违约时继续支付。为什么回收出现 $h$？它正是在违约事件发生时支付。

将三部分加起来乘 $P$，再使用 $Pmc/(m+r)=\alpha V_B$，最终：

$$
D(V;V_B)=P\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}
-\left[P\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}-\alpha V_B\right](V_B/V)^{h_{m+r}}.
$$

这与讲义更长的展开式完全等价。注意 $D(V;V_B)$ 是市场价值，$P$ 是面值；$h$ 在这段是期望 $H(m+r)$，$h_{m+r}$ 则是幂指数，别只凭字母形状混读。

**直觉检查。**令 $V=V_B$，两种幂都等于 1，得到 $v=D=\alpha V_B$，股权为零。说明立即违约时，企业价值就是债权人可收回的资产，没有未来税盾和股东剩余。

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Detailed solution <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">详细解答。</span></details>

**(a) Derive the barrier.** The formulas from Question 4 show <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）推导边界。第 4 题的公式给出：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
S(V;V_B)={}&V+\frac{\kappa\rho P}r-\left[\frac{\kappa\rho P}r+(1-\alpha)V_B\right]\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^{h_r}\\
&-P\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}+\left[P\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}-\alpha V_B\right]\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^{h_{m+r}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Hold the candidate barrier $V_B$ fixed when differentiating with respect to current assets $V$. For $k>0$, $\partial_V(V_B/V)^k=-(k/V)(V_B/V)^k$. Hence <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对当前资产 V 求导时固定候选边界 VB。对于 k＞0，∂V(VB／V)^k＝−(k／V)(VB／V)^k。因此：</span></details>

$$
\frac{\partial S}{\partial V}=1+\frac{h_r}V\left[\frac{\kappa\rho P}r+(1-\alpha)V_B\right]\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^{h_r}-\frac{h_{m+r}}V\left[P\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}-\alpha V_B\right]\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^{h_{m+r}}.
$$

At $V=V_B$, both powers equal one. Smooth pasting gives <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">在 V＝VB 处，两项幂均为 1。光滑粘贴给出：</span></details>

$$
0=V_B+h_r\left[\frac{\kappa\rho P}r+(1-\alpha)V_B\right]-h_{m+r}\left[P\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}-\alpha V_B\right].
$$

Collect the terms in $V_B$: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">合并 VB 项：</span></details>

$$
V_B[1+(1-\alpha)h_r+\alpha h_{m+r}]=P\left[\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}h_{m+r}-\frac{\kappa\rho}rh_r\right].
$$

Therefore, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，</span></details>

$$
V_B^*=\epsilon P,\quad\epsilon=\frac{\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}h_{m+r}-\frac{\kappa\rho}rh_r}{1+(1-\alpha)h_r+\alpha h_{m+r}}.
$$

**(b) Validity audit of the supplied inequality.** The requested bound is not valid for all positive model parameters stated in the problem; therefore it cannot be rigorously proved without additional assumptions. The original solution attempts to replace the denominator by $\alpha h_{m+r}$ and then concludes a smaller bound with $\alpha r+m$; that last comparison does not follow. A concrete counterexample consistent with $r>\kappa\rho$, $0<\alpha<1$, and $m>0$ is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）核查所给不等式的有效性。题目要求的界并非对全部给定正参数都成立，因此没有额外假设就无法严格证明。原解尝试将分母替换成 αhm+r，随后得出含 αr＋m 的更小上界，但最后一步比较并不成立。以下反例满足 r＞κρ、0＜α＜1、m＞0：</span></details>

$$
r=0.05,\quad\delta=0.03,\quad\sigma=0.20,\quad\rho=0.06,\quad\kappa=0.25,\quad\alpha=0.50,\quad m=1.
$$

Then $\xi=0$, $h_r=\sqrt{2.5}=1.581139$, $h_{m+r}=\sqrt{52.5}=7.245688$, and <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">则 ξ＝0、hr＝√2.5＝1.581139、hm+r＝√52.5＝7.245688，且：</span></details>

$$
\epsilon\approx1.263593,\qquad\frac{\rho+m}{\alpha r+m}=\frac{1.06}{1.025}\approx1.034146.
$$

Thus $\epsilon>(\rho+m)/(\alpha r+m)$, contradicting the requested inequality. The counterexample also has $\epsilon>0$ and satisfies $\kappa\rho=0.015<r$. **What can be proved without the missing assumption?** If $0\leq\kappa\leq1$, $0<\rho<r/\kappa$, $m>0$, and $0<\alpha\leq1$, then $h_{m+r}>h_r>0$ and <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因而 ε＞(ρ＋m)／(αr＋m)，与要求证明的不等式矛盾。该反例还满足 ε＞0 及 κρ＝0.015＜r。缺少额外假设时能证明什么？若 0≤κ≤1、0＜ρ＜r／κ、m＞0、0＜α≤1，则 hm+r＞hr＞0，且：</span></details>

$$
\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}>\frac{\kappa\rho}r\quad\text{if, for example, }0<\rho\leq r\text{ and }0\leq\kappa<1,
$$

which ensures the numerator is positive under the displayed coefficient restriction. Since the denominator exceeds $\alpha h_{m+r}$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">这保证在上述系数限制下分子为正。由于分母大于 αhm+r，</span></details>

$$
0<\epsilon<\frac{m+\rho}{\alpha(m+r)}
$$

holds whenever the numerator is positive. This weaker upper bound is correct; it is not the stronger upper bound stated in the original exam. $$
\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}h_{m+r}>\frac{\kappa\rho}rh_r.

Any claim of the stronger bound needs an additional parameter restriction that ensures it. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">若要主张更强的上界成立，必须补充能保证其成立的参数限制。</span></details>

### 中文详细讲解

**这题有两个性质完全不同的小问。**（a）是有效的微分计算；（b）要求的上界在所列一般条件下不成立，正确做法是解释反例，而不是补出一段错误证明。原 PDF 的修订版已经明确保留了这项审查。

**（a）先固定边界，再对当前资产 $V$ 求偏导。**令：

$$
a=h_r,\quad b=h_{r+m},\quad
k=\frac{\kappa\rho}{r},\quad d=\frac{m+\rho}{m+r}.
$$

这几个简记对应原式的重复系数。因为边界 $V_B$ 此时是常数：

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial V}(V_B/V)^a
=-\frac{a}{V}(V_B/V)^a.
$$

代入 $S=v-D$ 后，先求导再令 $V=V_B$，两个幂都变成 1。边界斜率为：

$$
S_V(V_B;V_B)
=1+(1-\alpha)a+\alpha b+\frac{P}{V_B}(ka-db).
$$

令这个斜率为零、整理边界，得到：

$$
V_B^*=\epsilon P,
\qquad
\epsilon=\frac{db-ka}{1+(1-\alpha)a+\alpha b}.
$$

平滑拼接 $S_V=0$ 与“把边界当决策变量直接令 $S_{V_B}=0$”不是同一个操作。此问是在求平滑拼接候选边界，不应把算出候选值当成完整证明所有约束下都最优。

**（b）逐字辨认题目要证明的上界。**它写的是：

$$
\epsilon<\frac{\rho+m}{\alpha r+m},
$$

而不是 $\epsilon<(\rho+m)/[\alpha(r+m)]$。注意 $\alpha r+m$ 与 $\alpha(r+m)$ 差一个 $m$ 是否也乘 $\alpha$；后者分母更小、给出的上界更宽松。

取原答案给出的参数 $r=0.05,\delta=0.03,\sigma=0.20,\rho=0.06,\kappa=0.25,\alpha=0.5,m=1$，复算得到：

$$
\epsilon=1.26359332,
\qquad \frac{\rho+m}{\alpha r+m}=1.03414634.
$$

左边已经大于右边，所以题目所写的一般命题不能成立。参数同时满足 $\kappa\rho=0.015<0.05=r$，并非简单违反税盾条件的伪反例。

**能正确得到什么？**在通常条件 $r,m,\rho,\sigma>0$、$0\leq\kappa<1$、$0<\alpha\leq1$、$\kappa\rho<r$ 下，有 $b>a>0$。还可以比较：

$$
d-k=\frac{(1-\kappa)\rho r+m(r-\kappa\rho)}{r(r+m)}>0.
$$

所以分子 $db-ka=d(b-a)+(d-k)a>0$；再利用分母大于 $\alpha b$，得到正确的较弱界：

$$
0<\epsilon<\frac{d}{\alpha}
=\frac{\rho+m}{\alpha(r+m)}.
$$

**结论：**（a）的边界表达式成立；（b）原题的更强界不能在现有一般条件下硬证。较弱界也不能自动推出单债回收 $c\leq1$；回收可行性仍需独立检查。
