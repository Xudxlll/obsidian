## 第 1 题｜四状态评级迁移

来源：[[AMA572 Fixed-Income and Credit Risk/Midterm/附件/AMA572_practice_questions_solutions.pdf#page=2|原题 PDF p.2]] · [[AMA572 Fixed-Income and Credit Risk/Midterm/附件/AMA572_practice_questions_solutions.pdf#page=10|原答案 PDF p.10–11]]。

> [!important] 老师对评级迁移题的要求（同类题通用）

>
> **字幕依据：**[[AMA572 Fixed-Income and Credit Risk/附件/授课字幕/midterm_字幕.srt|期中复习字幕]]，00:08:00–00:08:16（段72–74）；01:10:12–01:13:58（段363–388）；01:20:37–01:24:28（段438–481）。

### 题目

#### Question 1 (20 marks) <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 1 题（20 分）。</span></details>

There are four credit classes $A,B,C,D$, with $D$ the absorbing default state. At time 0 there are 24 firms in each of $A,B,C$, and none in $D$. Observe the firms over one year. The only transitions are: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">有 A、B、C、D 四个信用等级，D 为违约吸收状态。零时刻 A、B、C 各有 24 家企业，D 无企业。观察一年，只有以下迁移：</span></details>

(i) At the end of month 2, two originally $B$-rated firms move to $A$, and one other originally $B$-rated firm moves to $C$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（i）第 2 个月末，两家原 B 级企业转至 A，另一家原 B 级企业转至 C。</span></details>

(ii) At the end of month 4, three originally $A$-rated firms move to $B$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（ii）第 4 个月末，三家原 A 级企业转至 B。</span></details>

(iii) At the end of month 8, two originally $C$-rated firms move to $B$, and three other originally $C$-rated firms default. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（iii）第 8 个月末，两家原 C 级企业转至 B，另外三家原 C 级企业违约。</span></details>

(iv) At the end of month 11, two other originally $C$-rated firms default. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（iv）第 11 个月末，另外两家原 C 级企业违约。</span></details>

All firms mentioned are distinct. Every migrating firm remains in its new state until year-end. All other firms retain their initial rating. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">上述企业均不相同。所有迁移企业均在新状态保持至年末，其余企业维持初始等级。</span></details>

(a) Compute the empirical one-year transition matrix $\widehat P_{\mathrm{empirical}}$, using the order $A,B,C,D$. [5 marks] <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）按 A、B、C、D 排序，计算经验一年期迁移矩阵 P̂empirical。（5 分）</span></details>

(b) Compute the total exposure times $N_A,N_B,N_C$ in firm-years, the transition counts $M_{ij}$, and the generator estimator $\widehat\Lambda$, with off-diagonal entries $\widehat\lambda_{ij}=M_{ij}/N_i$. [9 marks] <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）计算以企业年计的总暴露时间 NA、NB、NC、迁移次数 Mij，以及非对角元素为 λ̂ij＝Mij／Ni 的生成矩阵估计 Λ̂。（9 分）</span></details>

(c) Compute $\widehat P_{\mathrm{generator}}(1)=\exp(\widehat\Lambda)$. Explain why its $(A,D)$ entry is positive even though no originally $A$-rated firm defaulted in the sample. [6 marks] <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（c）计算 P̂generator(1)＝exp(Λ̂)。解释为何样本中没有原 A 级企业违约，但矩阵 (A，D) 元素仍为正。（6 分）</span></details>

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Solution to Question 1 <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 1 题解答。</span></details>

**(a) Empirical transition matrix.** The empirical estimator compares each firm’s initial rating with its year-end rating; it ignores transition times. The initial $A$ cohort has 21 firms remaining in $A$ and 3 ending in $B$. The initial $B$ cohort has 2 ending in $A$, 21 in $B$, and 1 in $C$. The initial $C$ cohort has 2 ending in $B$, 17 in $C$, and 5 in $D$. Therefore <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）经验迁移矩阵。经验估计比较企业初始与年末等级，忽略迁移时间。原 A 队列有 21 家留 A、3 家至 B；原 B 队列有 2 家至 A、21 家留 B、1 家至 C；原 C 队列有 2 家至 B、17 家留 C、5 家至 D。因此：</span></details>

$$
\widehat P_{\mathrm{empirical}}=\begin{pmatrix}21/24&3/24&0&0\\2/24&21/24&1/24&0\\0&2/24&17/24&5/24\\0&0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The last row is imposed by the assumption that default is absorbing. It cannot be estimated as a cohort frequency because there were no initially defaulted firms. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">最后一行由违约吸收状态假设给定。因初始没有违约企业，不能用队列频率估计此行。</span></details>

**(b) Exposure times and generator.** Exposure includes time spent in a rating by both initial members and incoming firms. Months must be divided by 12 to obtain firm-years: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）暴露时间与生成矩阵。暴露包括初始成员及后来进入企业在该等级的停留时间。月份除以 12 才得到企业年：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_A&=21+3\frac4{12}+2\frac{10}{12}=\frac{71}3=23.666667,\\
N_B&=21+3\frac2{12}+3\frac8{12}+2\frac4{12}=\frac{145}6=24.166667,\\
N_C&=17+5\frac8{12}+2\frac{11}{12}+1\frac{10}{12}=23.
\end{aligned}
$$

For example, the two firms arriving in $A$ at month 2 contribute $2(12-2)/12$ firm-years to $N_A$, while the three firms leaving $A$ at month 4 contribute $3(4/12)$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">例如，第 2 个月进入 A 的两家企业对 NA 贡献 2(12−2)／12 企业年，第 4 个月离开 A 的三家企业贡献 3(4／12)。</span></details>

The nonzero transition counts are <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">非零迁移次数为：</span></details>

$$
M_{AB}=3,\quad M_{BA}=2,\quad M_{BC}=1,\quad M_{CB}=2,\quad M_{CD}=5.
$$

All other off-diagonal counts are zero. Dividing by the exposure of the origin state and setting each diagonal to the negative sum of its off-diagonal row gives, in units of $\mathrm{year}^{-1}$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其他非对角次数均为零。除以迁出状态的暴露时间，并令对角元素为该行非对角元素之和的负数，得到（单位：年⁻¹）：</span></details>

$$
\widehat\Lambda=\begin{pmatrix}-9/71&9/71&0&0\\12/145&-18/145&6/145&0\\0&2/23&-7/23&5/23\\0&0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Each row sums to zero and all off-diagonal entries are nonnegative. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">各行之和为零，全部非对角元素非负。</span></details>

#### Solution to Question 1 (continued) <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 1 题解答（续）。</span></details>

**(c) Generator-based transition probabilities.** With the annual time unit, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（c）基于生成矩阵的迁移概率。以年为时间单位，</span></details>

$$
\widehat P_{\mathrm{generator}}(1)=e^{\widehat\Lambda}=I+\widehat\Lambda+\frac{\widehat\Lambda^2}{2!}+\cdots.
$$

Numerically, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">数值上，</span></details>

$$
\widehat P_{\mathrm{generator}}(1)\approx\begin{pmatrix}0.885575&0.112075&0.002184&0.000166\\0.073171&0.889392&0.033526&0.003911\\0.002996&0.070453&0.739016&0.187534\\0&0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The unrounded row sums are 1; small discrepancies in the printed row sums are due to rounding. Although $\widehat\lambda_{AD}=0$, the fitted homogeneous continuous-time chain allows paths <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">未舍入时各行之和为 1，印出数值的小偏差来自舍入。虽然 λ̂AD＝0，但拟合的齐次连续时间链允许一年内存在路径：</span></details>

$$
A\longrightarrow B\longrightarrow C\longrightarrow D
$$

within one year. Every rate on this path is strictly positive, so the probability of such a path is positive. This explains the positive $(A,D)$ entry. The generator estimator uses the timing information to infer transition rates and permits multiple transitions in a future year. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">该路径上的每个迁移率都严格为正，因此路径概率为正，解释了 (A，D) 元素为正。生成矩阵估计利用时间信息推断迁移率，允许未来一年发生多次迁移。</span></details>

**Useful extension.** Under the same homogeneous model, the two-year transition matrix is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">有用的延伸：同一齐次模型下，两年期迁移矩阵为：</span></details>

$$
P(2)=e^{2\widehat\Lambda}=(e^{\widehat\Lambda})^2,
$$

which is approximately <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">约为：</span></details>

$$
\begin{pmatrix}0.792450&0.199084&0.007306&0.001160\\0.129977&0.801580&0.054754&0.013689\\0.010023&0.115062&0.548514&0.326401\\0&0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus the fitted two-year default probability for a firm currently rated $C$ is 0.326401. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，当前 C 级企业拟合的两年违约概率为 0.326401。</span></details>

**Common errors.** Dividing a count by the initial population estimates a cohort probability, not a continuous-time transition rate. Ignoring time spent in a rating by incoming firms gives the wrong generator. Finally, $e^{\widehat\Lambda}$ must be computed as a matrix exponential. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">常见错误：次数除以初始企业数估计的是队列概率，而非连续时间迁移率；忽略后来进入企业的停留时间会导致错误生成矩阵；e^Λ̂ 必须按矩阵指数计算。</span></details>

### 中文详细讲解

**这题的工作顺序固定：期末人数 → 累计停留时间 → 迁移次数 → 生成矩阵 → 矩阵指数。**本题明确说各公司不同且迁入后留到年底，所以没有身份归属的歧义。

**（a）经验概率只按最初的群体统计。**A 初始 24 家，21 留 A、3 去 B；B 中 2 去 A、21 留 B、1 去 C；C 中 2 去 B、17 留 C、5 违约。将这些数分别除以 24，得到原答案的经验矩阵。最后一行来自吸收态假设，不能因为期初 D 是零家就计算 $0/0$。

**（b）逐组解释累计时间。**

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_A&=21+3\frac4{12}+2\frac{10}{12}=\frac{71}{3},\\
N_B&=21+3\frac2{12}+3\frac8{12}+2\frac4{12}=\frac{145}{6},\\
N_C&=17+5\frac8{12}+2\frac{11}{12}+1\frac{10}{12}=23.
\end{aligned}
$$

例如 $N_B$ 的四项是：原来一直留下的 21 家；2 月离开 B 的三家在 B 待两个月；4 月从 A 迁来的三家在 B 待八个月；8 月从 C 迁来的两家在 B 待四个月。这里每项是“人数×在场年数”，不是到期概率。

迁移次数为 $M_{AB}=3,M_{BA}=2,M_{BC}=1,M_{CB}=2,M_{CD}=5$。每次除以出发评级的暴露时间，例如：

$$
\widehat\lambda_{AB}=\frac3{71/3}=\frac9{71},
\qquad
\widehat\lambda_{BC}=\frac1{145/6}=\frac6{145}.
$$

对角项设为同一行其余强度之和的负数。这样既保证离开一个评级的总概率流有来源，也保证生成矩阵行和为零。

**（c）用矩阵指数得到未来一年概率。**原答案矩阵已复算，例如：

$$
P_{AD}(1)=0.000165724,
\qquad P_{CD}(1)=0.187534329.
$$

$$
B_T(y)=4.2\sum_{j=1}^T e^{-yj}+100e^{-yT}.

|---|---:|---:|---:|

| 4 年 | 106.272047 | 97.452415 | 8.819632 |

$$
F_2(0.5)=4.2e^{-0.025(0.5)}+104.2e^{-0.025(1.5)}=104.5127.

$$
3.577860932=134.0554861P_1,
\qquad P_1\approx0.0266894033=2.66894033\%.
$$

**（c）长债必须按日历年份分配参数。**四年债在 0.5、1.5 年违约的情景仍属于第 1–2 年，乘 $P_1$；在 2.5、3.5 年违约才乘 $P_2$。对原答案完整表加总得到：

8.819631803=140.170764363P_1+127.517522855P_2.

因此：

$$
P_2=\frac{8.819631803-140.170764363P_1}{127.517522855}

$$
d\log V_t=\frac{dV_t}{V_t}-\frac1{2V_t^2}(dV_t)^2=\left(r-\frac12\sigma^2\right)dt+\sigma\,dW_t.
$$

Integrating from 0 to $T$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">从 0 至 T 积分，</span></details>

$$
\log V_T=\log V_0+\left(r-\frac12\sigma^2\right)T+\sigma W_T.
$$

Since $W_T\sim N(0,T)$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">由于 WT∼N(0，T)，</span></details>

$$
\log V_T\sim N\left(\log V_0+\left(r-\frac12\sigma^2\right)T,\ \sigma^2T\right).
$$

Define <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">定义：</span></details>

$$
d_1=\frac{\log(V_0/D)+(r+\sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma\sqrt T},\quad d_2=d_1-\sigma\sqrt T.
$$

Standardization gives <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">标准化得到：</span></details>

#### Solution to Question 4 <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 4 题解答。</span></details>

**(a) First-passage transform.** The asset process has the solution <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）首次穿越变换。资产过程的解为：</span></details>

$$
V_t=V\exp\left[\left(r-\delta-\frac12\sigma^2\right)t+\sigma W_t\right].
$$

Hence $\tau$ is the first time the process $X_t=\mu t+\sigma W_t$ reaches $b=\log(V_B/V)<0$, where $\mu=r-\delta-\sigma^2/2=\sigma^2\xi$. The downward first-passage formula yields <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因而 τ 是 Xt＝μt＋σWt 首次到达 b＝log(VB／V)＜0 的时刻，其中 μ＝r−δ−σ²／2＝σ²ξ。向下首次穿越公式给出：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^*[e^{-s\tau}]&=\exp\left[\frac{\log(V_B/V)}{\sigma^2}(\sqrt{\mu^2+2\sigma^2s}+\mu)\right]\\
&=\exp\left[\log(V_B/V)\left(\sqrt{\xi^2+\frac{2s}{\sigma^2}}+\xi\right)\right]\\
&=\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^{\sqrt{\xi^2+2s/\sigma^2}+\xi}.
\end{aligned}
$$

For $s>0$, the exponent is positive, and the transform lies strictly between 0 and 1 when $V>V_B$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">s＞0 时幂指数为正，当 V＞VB 时变换值严格位于 0 与 1 之间。</span></details>

**(b) Total firm value.** Pathwise, with the same convention when $\tau=\infty$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）企业总价值。逐路径计算，并对 τ＝∞ 采用相同约定，</span></details>

$$
\int_0^\tau e^{-rt}\,dt=\frac{1-e^{-r\tau}}r.
$$

With $q=\sqrt{\xi^2+2r/\sigma^2}+\xi$, substitution gives <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">令 q＝√(ξ²＋2r／σ²)＋ξ，代入得：</span></details>

&=V+\frac{\kappa\rho}rP-\left[\frac{\kappa\rho}rP+(1-\alpha)V_B\right]\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^q.
\end{aligned}
$$

The first correction is the value of tax deductions earned until default; the second is the discounted bankruptcy loss. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第一项调整是违约前获得税收抵扣的价值，第二项是贴现破产损失。</span></details>

-\left[\frac{\kappa\rho P}{r}+(1-\alpha)V_B\right](V_B/V)^q.
$$

**（c）总债务积分要先分三笔钱，再交换积分顺序。**对已给定的一条违约路径，单只债的本金只在 $T<\tau$ 时支付；回收只在 $T\geq\tau$ 时支付；票息只持续到 $\min(T,\tau)$。三个分量都非负，可以用 Tonelli 定理交换期望和期限积分。

本金与回收直接按 $T$ 的区间切开。票息要注意：只有剩余期限 $T\geq t$ 的债券，才能在票息时间 $t$ 支付，因此内层期限加权从 $t$ 积到无穷，得到 $e^{-mt}$。

对路径积分后，单位总面值的三项为：

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{\mathrm{principal}}&=\frac{m}{m+r}(1-e^{-(m+r)\tau}),\\
I_{\mathrm{coupon}}&=\frac{\rho}{m+r}(1-e^{-(m+r)\tau}),\\
I_{\mathrm{recovery}}&=\frac{mc}{m+r}e^{-(m+r)\tau}.
\end{aligned}
$$

取期望，乘 $P$，再用 $Pmc/(m+r)=\alpha V_B$。令 $h=\sqrt{\xi^2+2(r+m)/\sigma^2}+\xi$，得到：

$$
D(V;V_B)=\frac{\rho+m}{r+m}P
-\left[\frac{\rho+m}{r+m}P-\alpha V_B\right](V_B/V)^h.
$$

**（d）把资产状态移到边界，检验经济含义。**$V=V_B$ 时所有幂为 1，税盾来不及产生，企业立即违约：

$$
v(V_B;V_B)=D(V_B;V_B)=\alpha V_B,
\qquad S(V_B;V_B)=0.
$$

**易错点：**$V_B$ 是资产水平，$\tau$ 是到达它的时间；$q,h$ 在本题是幂指数，不是年度违约概率或风险率。单债回收比例 $c\leq1$ 也是独立的模型条件。

#### Solution to Question 5 <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 5 题解答。</span></details>

**(a) Differentiation at fixed barrier.** For any constant exponent $a$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）固定边界求导。对任意常数指数 a，</span></details>

$$
S_V(V;V_B)=1+\frac qV\left[\frac{\kappa\rho}rP+(1-\alpha)V_B\right]\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^q-\frac hV\left[\frac{\rho+m}{r+m}P-\alpha V_B\right]\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^h.
$$

At the boundary this becomes <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">在边界处变为：</span></details>

$$
S_V(V_B;V_B)=1+(1-\alpha)q+\alpha h+\frac P{V_B}\left(\frac{\kappa\rho}rq-\frac{\rho+m}{r+m}h\right).
$$

**(b) Smooth-pasting equation.** Set the boundary derivative to zero and multiply by $V_B$: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）光滑粘贴方程。令边界导数为零，并乘 VB：</span></details>

$$
[1+(1-\alpha)q+\alpha h]V_B=P\left[\frac{\rho+m}{r+m}h-\frac{\kappa\rho}rq\right].
$$

Because the coefficient on $V_B$ is strictly positive, the candidate is unique and is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因 VB 的系数严格为正，候选解唯一，为：</span></details>

$$
V_B^*=\epsilon P,\quad\epsilon=\frac{\frac{\rho+m}{r+m}h-\frac{\kappa\rho}rq}{1+(1-\alpha)q+\alpha h}.
$$

The operation is a derivative with respect to the current state $V$ followed by evaluation at the barrier. It is not a derivative with respect to $V_B$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">此操作先对当前状态 V 求导，再在边界处求值，不是对 VB 求导。</span></details>

**(c) Positivity and upper bound.** Since $m>0$, $h>q>0$. To compare the coefficients, write <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（c）正性与上界。由于 m＞0，有 h＞q＞0。为比较系数，写为：</span></details>

This is a weighted average of $\rho/r$ and 1. If $\rho/r\leq1$, then $(\rho+m)/(r+m)>\rho/r>\kappa\rho/r$. If $\rho/r>1$, then $(\rho+m)/(r+m)>1>\kappa\rho/r$, using $\kappa\rho<r$. ### 中文详细讲解

**本题任务是求平滑拼接候选边界及其范围，并做数值检验。**题目最后已经限定：不要求完整证明该候选解解决全部有限责任优化问题。

**（a）对谁求导必须说清楚。**固定 $V_B$ 和 $P$，对当前资产 $V$ 求偏导。任何常指数 $a$ 都有：

$$
\partial_V(V_B/V)^a=-\frac{a}{V}(V_B/V)^a.
$$

令 $k=\kappa\rho/r$、$d=(\rho+m)/(r+m)$，求导后再代 $V=V_B$，得到：

$$
S_V(V_B;V_B)
=1+(1-\alpha)q+\alpha h+\frac{P}{V_B}(kq-dh).
$$

**（b）令边界斜率为零，整理出边界。**把上式乘 $V_B$ 后，将所有含 $V_B$ 的项移在一起：

$$
[1+(1-\alpha)q+\alpha h]V_B=P(dh-kq).
$$

因此：

$$
V_B^*=\epsilon P,
\qquad \epsilon=\frac{dh-kq}{1+(1-\alpha)q+\alpha h}.
$$

这个比例不随 $P$ 变化，是下一题“改变债务规模时边界也同比例变化”的基础。

**（c）下界与上界分开证。**由 $m>0$，有 $h>q>0$。为了确认分子正，不能只说“参数都正”，因为分子里有减号；应明确比较：

$$
d-k=\frac{(1-\kappa)\rho r+m(r-\kappa\rho)}{r(r+m)}>0.
$$

这是题目 $0<\kappa<1$、$\kappa\rho<r$ 的用途。于是 $dh-kq=d(h-q)+(d-k)q>0$，而分母也正，因此 $\epsilon>0$。

对上界，分子 $dh-kq<dh$，分母大于 $\alpha h$，所以：

$$
\epsilon<\frac{dh}{1+(1-\alpha)q+\alpha h}
<\frac{dh}{\alpha h}
=\frac{\rho+m}{\alpha(r+m)}.
$$

这是本卷正确的较弱上界；不要与修订版卷二第 5(b) 的 $\alpha r+m$ 分母混为一谈。

**（d）按由内到外的顺序代参数。**先算 $\xi=0.25$，再算 $q=1.850781059,h=3.794361720$，以及 $k=0.30,d=1.04$。然后：

$$
\epsilon=0.8441527062,
\qquad V_B^*=80\epsilon=67.53221650,
\qquad c=0.6331145297.
$$

继续代入第 4 题的企业与债务公式：

$$
v=162.3524193,
\quad D=81.1337618,
\quad S=v-D=81.2186575.
$$

分别检查 $67.5322<150$ 和 $0<c<1$。**$\epsilon$ 上界本身不能自动证明 $c\leq1$**：后者要求 $\epsilon\leq m/[\alpha(m+r)]$，必须额外检验。本题数值确实满足。

$$

$$

$$

$$

\qquad s=2.72982338\%=272.9823\text{ bp/年}.
$$

债股和为 120。收益率公式中的 $T=2$ 必须除掉，否则把两年总对数收益误当年化收益。

\sigma_SS_0=\sigma V_0S_V(0,V_0)

$$

#### Solution to Question 7 <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 7 题解答。</span></details>

**(a) Strict concavity.** For clarity put $k=\kappa\rho/r>0$ and $H=[k+(1-\alpha)\epsilon](\epsilon/V)^q>0$. Then <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）严格凹性。为清晰起见，令 k＝κρ／r＞0、H＝[k＋(1−α)ε](ε／V)^q＞0。则：</span></details>

$$
v(V;\epsilon P)=V+kP-HP^{q+1}.
$$

For $P>0$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对 P＞0，</span></details>

$$
\frac{dv}{dP}=k-(q+1)HP^q,\quad\frac{d^2v}{dP^2}=-q(q+1)HP^{q-1}<0.
$$

The strict inequality follows from $q,H,P>0$, even if $q<1$. Hence there is at most one maximizer. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">q、H、P＞0 保证严格不等式成立，即使 q＜1 也如此。因此最多存在一个最大化点。</span></details>

**(b) Optimal debt and interior feasibility.** Setting the first derivative to zero gives <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）最优债务与内部可行性。令一阶导数为零，得：</span></details>

$$
(P^*)^q=\frac k{(q+1)H}.
$$

Substituting for $H$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">代入 H，</span></details>

$$
P^*=\frac V\epsilon\left[\frac{\kappa\rho/r}{(q+1)[\kappa\rho/r+(1-\alpha)\epsilon]}\right]^{1/q}.
$$

The bracket is strictly between 0 and 1 because its denominator exceeds its positive numerator. Therefore $0<P^*<V/\epsilon$, so the implied barrier satisfies $\epsilon P^*<V$. Strict concavity proves that this stationary point is the unique global maximizer on the stated domain. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">方括号内的正分子小于分母，所以其值严格位于 0 与 1 之间。因此 0＜P*＜V／ε，隐含边界满足 εP*＜V。严格凹性证明此驻点是给定定义域上的唯一全局最大化点。</span></details>

A useful value check follows from the first-order condition: $H(P^*)^{q+1}=kP^*/(q+1)$. Thus <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">一阶条件给出有用的价值检查：H(P*)^(q＋1)＝kP*／(q＋1)，因此：</span></details>

$$
v(V;\epsilon P^*)=V+\frac q{q+1}kP^*.
$$

$$

These quantities obey the barrier feasibility condition. The same recovery coefficient as in Question 5 is $c=0.6331145$, since $V_B^*/P^*=\epsilon$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">这些数值满足边界可行性条件。因 VB*／P*＝ε，回收系数与第 5 题相同，为 c＝0.6331145。</span></details>

The smooth-pasting derivative is $S_V$ at a fixed barrier and fixed debt level, evaluated at the default state. The second-stage derivative is of the total firm value with respect to the debt level, allowing the adopted barrier to change through $V_B^*(P)=\epsilon P$. The two derivatives address different decisions. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">光滑粘贴导数是在固定边界、固定债务水平下的 SV，并在违约状态求值。第二阶段导数是企业总价值对债务水平的导数，允许已采用边界随 VB*(P)＝εP 变化。二者对应不同决策。</span></details>

### 中文详细讲解

**本题解决融资规模，而不是再求一次平滑拼接。**第 5 题已经给出股东的边界规则 $V_B^*(P)=\epsilon P$；现在要预见这个规则，选择企业总价值最大的 $P$。

**（a）先把函数写成容易求导的幂函数。**令：

$$
k=\frac{\kappa\rho}{r}>0,
\qquad H=[k+(1-\alpha)\epsilon](\epsilon/V)^q>0.
$$

因 $V$ 与模型参数固定、$\epsilon$ 不依赖 $P$，目标就是：

$$
v(P)=V+kP-HP^{q+1}.
$$

求一阶、二阶导：

$$
v'(P)=k-(q+1)HP^q,
\qquad v''(P)=-q(q+1)HP^{q-1}<0.
$$

$P>0$ 时，即使 $0<q<1$，$P^{q-1}$ 仍为正；所以严格凹性成立，驻点至多一个。

**（b）先求驻点，再验证它在可行区间内。**令一阶导为零：

$$
P^*=\frac{V}{\epsilon}
\left[\frac{k}{(q+1)[k+(1-\alpha)\epsilon]}\right]^{1/q}.
$$

括号内分子为正，分母大于分子，所以括号及其 $1/q$ 次方都在 0 与 1 之间。由此：

$$
0<P^*<V/\epsilon,
\qquad V_B^*(P^*)=\epsilon P^*<V.
$$

严格凹性加上内点可行性，才完整说明这是题目所列区间内唯一的全局最大值，而不只是把导数设成零。

**（c）代入第 5 题参数。**

$$
P^*=67.1293172,
\qquad V_B^*=56.6673947,
\qquad v_{\max}=163.0744873.
$$

可用一阶条件 $H(P^*)^{q+1}=kP^*/(q+1)$ 独立检验最大值：

$$
v_{\max}=V+\frac{q}{q+1}kP^*.
$$

第 5 题固定 $P=80$ 时企业价值约为 162.3524；现在允许调到最优规模，价值升到 163.0745，与最大化目标一致。

**两种导数的区别。**第 5 题先固定边界和债务，对当前资产状态求 $S_V$，再到边界评估；本题对债务规模求 $dv/dP$，边界通过 $V_B=\epsilon P$ 随 $P$ 变化。前者的股东违约决策与后者的企业融资决策不能混用。当前参数下 $c=(m+r)\alpha\epsilon/m=0.6331145$，不随 $P$ 变化，回收可行性也保持满足。

**(c) Break-even spread.** Equate the buyer’s regular and accrued premiums to the seller’s protection: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（c）盈亏平衡利差。令买方常规及应计保费等于卖方保护支付：</span></details>

$$

Using unrounded sums, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">使用未舍入的总和，</span></details>

$$
s=0.0167838315=1.678383\%=167.8383\text{ basis points}.
$$

The full annual premium on 10 million notional, conditional on survival to that payment date, is $10{,}000{,}000s=167{,}838.31$. On mid-year default, the accrued premium is half this amount. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对 1,000 万名义本金，若存续至支付日，全年保费为 10,000,000s＝167,838.31。若年中违约，应计保费为该金额的一半。</span></details>

**(d) Effect of omitting accrual.** Omitting accrual removes a positive term from the premium-leg coefficient. For an unchanged protection leg, the inferred spread is therefore too high. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（d）忽略应计保费的影响。忽略后保费腿系数少了一个正项，保护腿不变时，推断利差会偏高。</span></details>

### 中文详细讲解

**这是一份四年 CDS，必须保留应计保费。**本金 1,000 万先放一边，用单位名义本金做表；解出保费率后再乘本金。年度条件违约概率是 $p=0.025$，不是连续强度。

**（a）每行概率先算对。**

$$
Q_j=(1-p)^j,
\qquad d_j=(1-p)^{j-1}p.
$$

$Q_j$ 是存活至年末的概率，$d_j$ 是第 $j$ 年中违约的无条件概率。例如 $d_3=0.975^2\times0.025=0.023765625$。原答案列出四行；四行违约概率加第四年末存活概率必须等于 1。

**（b）把金额、概率、时间分开。**常规保费金额为 $s$，年末支付并乘 $Q_j$；应计保费金额为 $s/2$，年中违约时支付并乘 $d_j$；保护赔付金额为 $0.65$，也在年中违约时支付并乘 $d_j$。所以三列分别为：

$$
sQ_je^{-0.04j},
\qquad \tfrac12sd_je^{-0.04(j-1/2)},
\qquad 0.65d_je^{-0.04(j-1/2)}.
$$

例如第三年的应计保费系数为 $0.5\times0.023765625\times e^{-0.04\times2.5}=0.01075201$。这只是系数，还没有乘 $s$。

**（c）用现值相等求保费率。**加总后：

$$
A=3.406429725,
\qquad B=0.044554412,
\qquad C=0.057920736.
$$

因此：

$$
s=\frac{C}{A+B}
=0.01678383147
=1.678383147\%
=167.8383\text{ bp/年}.
$$

每个完整年度的名义保费金额为：

$$
10{,}000{,}000s=167{,}838.31.
$$

这是存活到付费日时需要支付的金额，不是每年无条件保证支付的金额；若年中违约，则补半年约 $83{,}919.16$。

**（d）漏掉应计保费会怎样？**保护腿 $C$ 不变，但分母从 $A+B$ 减少为 $A$，所以推算的 $s=C/A$ 偏高，本题约变成 $170.03$ bp。不能凭“少交了一笔钱”就反过来判断公平费率会更低。

本题的费率恰好与卷一五年 CDS 相同，并非串用了答案：它们的每年违约概率、回收率、利率和支付规则相同且平坦，各年现金流系数成同一比例。若风险随年限变化或支付规则改变，期限改变通常就不会继续给同一结果。

### 题目

#### Question 9 (20 marks) <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 9 题（20 分）。</span></details>

In the Duffie–Singleton model, recovery is a fraction $R$ of the market value immediately before default. For this question use $R$ for the recovery fraction, reserving $\delta$ for asset payout rates in the structural models. Let $r=0.03$, $R=0.40$, and let the deterministic risk-neutral default intensity be <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">Duffie–Singleton 模型中，回收为紧接违约前市场价值的比例 R。本题用 R 表示回收比例，保留 δ 表示结构式模型的资产支付率。令 r＝0.03、R＝0.40，确定性风险中性违约强度为：</span></details>

$$
\lambda(t)=\begin{cases}0.02,&0\leq t\leq2,\\0.05,&2<t\leq5.\end{cases}
$$

For a unit-face-value zero-coupon bond, use <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对单位面值零息债券，使用：</span></details>

$$
B(0,T)=\exp\left(-\int_0^T[r+(1-R)\lambda(u)]\,du\right).
$$

(a) Compute survival and cumulative default probabilities at years 2 and 5. [5 marks] <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）计算第 2、5 年的存续概率及累积违约概率。（5 分）</span></details>

(b) Compute the 2-year and 5-year bond prices, yields and credit spreads. [6 marks] <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）计算两年期、五年期债券价格、收益率和信用利差。（6 分）</span></details>

(c) Suppose these two yields and $R$ are known but both piecewise intensities are unknown. Derive the calibration equations and recover the intensities. [5 marks] <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（c）假设已知这两个收益率和 R，但两段强度未知。推导校准方程并求出强度。（5 分）</span></details>

(d) If $Q(T)=P^*(\tau>T)$, express the hazard rate using $Q'(T)$. Explain the role of $R$ in default probability versus bond valuation. [4 marks] <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（d）若 Q(T)＝P*(τ＞T)，用 Q′(T) 表示风险率，并解释 R 在违约概率与债券估值中的不同作用。（4 分）</span></details>

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Solution to Question 9 <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 9 题解答。</span></details>

**(a) Survival and cumulative default.** For a deterministic intensity, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）存续与累积违约。对于确定性强度，</span></details>

$$
Q(T)=\exp\left(-\int_0^T\lambda(u)\,du\right).
$$

The integrated intensities are <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">累积强度为：</span></details>

$$
\int_0^2\lambda(u)\,du=0.04,\quad\int_0^5\lambda(u)\,du=2(0.02)+3(0.05)=0.19.
$$

Hence <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，</span></details>

| $T$ | Survival $Q(T)$ | Cumulative default $1-Q(T)$ |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | $e^{-0.04}=0.960789$ | 0.039211 |
| 5 | $e^{-0.19}=0.826959$ | 0.173041 |

<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">表头依次为：期限 T、存续概率 Q(T)、累积违约概率 1−Q(T)。</span></details>


**(b) Bond prices, yields and spreads.** With $1-R=0.60$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）债券价格、收益率和利差。1−R＝0.60 时，</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
B(0,2)&=e^{-0.03(2)-0.60(0.04)}=e^{-0.084}=0.919431,\\
B(0,5)&=e^{-0.03(5)-0.60(0.19)}=e^{-0.264}=0.767974.
\end{aligned}
$$

For unit face value, $y(T)=-\log B(0,T)/T$, so <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">单位面值时 y(T)＝−log B(0，T)／T，因此：</span></details>

| $T$ | Price | Yield | Spread above $r$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 0.919431 | 4.20% | 1.20% = 120 bp |
| 5 | 0.767974 | 5.28% | 2.28% = 228 bp |

<details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">表头依次为：期限 T、价格、收益率、超过无风险利率 r 的利差；bp 表示基点。</span></details>


The spread is the loss fraction $1-R$ times the average intensity over the maturity, not necessarily the intensity applying in the final year. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">利差是损失比例 1−R 乘整个期限内的平均强度，不一定对应最后一年的强度。</span></details>

**(c) Calibration.** Let $\lambda_1$ apply over $(0,2]$ and $\lambda_2$ over $(2,5]$. Then <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（c）校准。令 λ1 适用于 (0，2]，λ2 适用于 (2，5]，则：</span></details>

$$
2[y(2)-r]=(1-R)2\lambda_1,\quad5[y(5)-r]=(1-R)(2\lambda_1+3\lambda_2).
$$

Thus <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，</span></details>

$$
\lambda_1=\frac{y(2)-r}{1-R}=0.02,\quad\lambda_2=\frac{5[y(5)-r]-2[y(2)-r]}{3(1-R)}=0.05.
$$

This calibration assumes the recovery fraction is known; prices alone do not identify recovery and intensity separately in this formula. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">此校准假定回收比例已知；在该公式中，单凭价格不能分别识别回收比例和强度。</span></details>

**(d) Hazard sign and recovery.** Because $Q$ is decreasing, the default density is $f_\tau(T)=-Q'(T)$. Where the derivative exists, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（d）风险率符号与回收。Q 递减，因此违约密度 fτ(T)＝−Q′(T)。在导数存在处，</span></details>

$$
\lambda(T)=\frac{f_\tau(T)}{Q(T)}=-\frac{Q'(T)}{Q(T)}.
$$

For a fixed specified intensity, recovery does not enter the survival or default probabilities. It affects the loss on default, hence bond valuation. The recovery convention here is a fraction of pre-default market value; it differs from recovery of face value in Questions 2 and 8. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">给定强度固定时，回收不影响存续或违约概率，而影响违约损失及债券估值。这里按违约前市值比例回收，不同于第 2、8 题的面值比例回收。</span></details>

### 中文详细讲解

**本题同时区分三件事：**违约概率取决于累计强度；债券价格还受利率和回收损失影响；市场利差反映整个期限的平均强度，不一定等于最后一年的强度。

**（a）先算累计强度，再求生存概率。**前两年强度 2%，后三年 5%。因此：

$$
I(2)=2\times0.02=0.04,
\qquad I(5)=2\times0.02+3\times0.05=0.19.
$$

确定性强度下 $Q(T)=e^{-I(T)}$，所以：

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q(2)&=0.960789439,&P^*(\tau\leq2)&=0.039210561,\\
Q(5)&=0.826959134,&P^*(\tau\leq5)&=0.173040866.
\end{aligned}
$$

不能直接把五年累计强度 0.19 当成五年违约概率 19%。

**（b）再把损失比例纳入 Duffie–Singleton 折现。**这里 $R=0.4$ 是违约前市场价值回收比例，$1-R=0.6$。因强度确定，期望公式简化为：

$$
B(0,T)=e^{-rT-(1-R)I(T)}.
$$

因此 $B(0,2)=e^{-0.084}=0.919431256$，$B(0,5)=e^{-0.264}=0.767973540$。零息债收益率是 $y(T)=-\ln B(0,T)/T$，结果：

| 到期 | 收益率 | 利差 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2 年 | 4.20% | 1.20%＝120 bp |
| 5 年 | 5.28% | 2.28%＝228 bp |

五年利差是 $0.6\times(0.19/5)$，不是简单取最后一段的 $0.6\times0.05$。

**（c）反过来校准：先早段，后晚段。**由 $T[y(T)-r]=(1-R)I(T)$，有：

$$
2[y(2)-r]=(1-R)2\lambda_1,
$$

$$
5[y(5)-r]=(1-R)(2\lambda_1+3\lambda_2).
$$

先解得 $\lambda_1=[y(2)-r]/(1-R)=0.02$；再扣除前两年，得到：

$$
\lambda_2=\frac{5[y(5)-r]-2[y(2)-r]}{3(1-R)}=0.05.
$$

这是精确确定性强度公式，与前面债价题明确采用的线性概率权重近似不同。还必须知道回收率；仅凭这些价格不能同时唯一识别回收率与所有强度。

**（d）风险率的负号来自生存函数递减。**密度 $f_\tau(T)=-Q'(T)$，所以在可微处：

$$
\lambda(T)=-\frac{Q'(T)}{Q(T)}.
$$

在强度分段切换点可分别讨论左右两侧。若强度已经固定，回收率不改变默认的违约到达概率，只改变违约损失和债券价格；若反过来从同一个价格校准，假设不同的回收率则会得到不同的隐含强度。这里的市场价值回收，也不能替换成 CDS 题的面值比例回收。
