### 题目

#### Question 1. Rating transitions and generator estimation [15 points] <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 1 题：评级迁移与生成矩阵估计（15 分）。</span></details>

There are three credit states: $A$, $B$, and default $D$. Default is absorbing. At time 0 there are 12 firms in state $A$ and 8 firms in state $B$. During the following year: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">有三种信用状态：A、B 和违约 D。违约是吸收状态（absorbing state）。零时刻有 12 家 A 级企业和 8 家 B 级企业。接下来一年内：</span></details>

- At month 3, two $A$-rated firms migrate to $B$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 3 个月，两家 A 级企业迁移至 B 级。</span></details>

- At month 6, one $B$-rated firm migrates to $A$, and one other $B$-rated firm defaults. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 6 个月，一家 B 级企业迁移至 A 级，另有一家 B 级企业违约。</span></details>

- At month 9, one $A$-rated firm defaults. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 9 个月，一家 A 级企业违约。</span></details>

- No other transitions occur. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">没有其他迁移。</span></details>

(a) Compute the empirical one-year transition matrix. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）计算经验一年期迁移矩阵（empirical transition matrix）。</span></details>

(b) Compute the total exposure times $N_A$ and $N_B$, measured in firm-years. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）计算总暴露时间（exposure times）NA 和 NB，以企业年为单位。</span></details>

(c) Estimate the generator $\widehat\Lambda$ using $\widehat\lambda_{ij}=M_{ij}/N_i$ for $i\ne j$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（c）对 i≠j，使用 λ̂ij＝Mij／Ni 估计生成矩阵 Λ̂。</span></details>

(d) Compute the generator-based one-year transition matrix $\widehat P_{\mathrm{gen}}=\exp(\widehat\Lambda)$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（d）计算基于生成矩阵的一年期迁移矩阵 P̂gen＝exp(Λ̂)。</span></details>

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Solution 1. Rating transitions and generator estimation <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 1 题解答：评级迁移与生成矩阵估计。</span></details>

**Solution.** <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">解答。</span></details>

The empirical estimator records each firm’s state at the beginning and end of the year. Among the 12 firms initially in $A$, nine finish in $A$, two finish in $B$, and one defaults. Among the 8 firms initially in $B$, one finishes in $A$, six finish in $B$, and one defaults. Therefore, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">经验估计量记录每家企业年初和年末的状态。最初 12 家 A 级企业中，9 家年末仍为 A，2 家为 B，1 家违约。最初 8 家 B 级企业中，1 家年末为 A，6 家为 B，1 家违约。因此：</span></details>

$$
\widehat P_{\mathrm{emp}}=\begin{pmatrix}9/12&2/12&1/12\\1/8&6/8&1/8\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}0.7500&0.1667&0.0833\\0.1250&0.7500&0.1250\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

For state $A$, the original $A$ firms contribute <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对状态 A，原 A 级企业贡献：</span></details>

$$
9(1)+2\left(\frac3{12}\right)+1\left(\frac9{12}\right)=10.25
$$

firm-years. The firm that migrates from $B$ to $A$ at month 6 contributes another $6/12$. Thus, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">企业年。第 6 个月从 B 迁至 A 的企业再贡献 6／12 企业年。因此：</span></details>

$$
N_A=10.25+0.50=10.75=\frac{43}4.
$$

For state $B$, the original $B$ firms contribute <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对状态 B，原 B 级企业贡献：</span></details>

$$
6(1)+2\left(\frac6{12}\right)=7
$$

firm-years. The two firms that migrate from $A$ to $B$ at month 3 contribute $2(9/12)=1.5$ firm-years. Hence, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">企业年。第 3 个月从 A 迁至 B 的两家企业贡献 2×(9／12)＝1.5 企业年。因此：</span></details>

$$
N_B=7+1.5=8.5=\frac{17}2.
$$

The observed transition counts are <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">观察到的迁移次数为：</span></details>

$$
M_{AB}=2,\quad M_{AD}=1,\quad M_{BA}=1,\quad M_{BD}=1.
$$

Consequently, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，</span></details>

$$
\widehat\lambda_{AB}=\frac2{43/4}=\frac8{43},\quad\widehat\lambda_{AD}=\frac1{43/4}=\frac4{43},
$$

and <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">以及：</span></details>

$$
\widehat\lambda_{BA}=\widehat\lambda_{BD}=\frac1{17/2}=\frac2{17}.
$$

The diagonal entries make each row sum to zero, so <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对角元素令各行之和为零，因此：</span></details>

$$
\widehat\Lambda=\begin{pmatrix}-12/43&8/43&4/43\\2/17&-4/17&2/17\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}\approx\begin{pmatrix}-0.27907&0.18605&0.09302\\0.11765&-0.23529&0.11765\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Exponentiating the matrix gives <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对矩阵取指数，得到：</span></details>

$$
\widehat P_{\mathrm{gen}}=e^{\widehat\Lambda}\approx\begin{pmatrix}0.76490&0.14439&0.09070\\0.09131&0.79888&0.10981\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Unlike the direct empirical estimator, the generator estimator uses the exact amount of time spent in each state and allows multi-step migrations within the year. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">不同于直接经验估计，生成矩阵估计使用各状态中的精确停留时间，并允许一年内发生多步迁移。</span></details>

### 中文详细讲解

**这题在考什么？**把“公司的评级变动记录”转成两种对象：一年后处于各评级的概率，以及每单位时间的迁移速率。它对应 Lecture 1。

**（a）先按最初属于哪一组，统计年底去了哪里。**经验矩阵只比较期初、期末，不使用月份。按配套答案的分组口径，初始 A 的 12 家最终为 A：9 家、B：2 家、D：1 家；初始 B 的 8 家最终为 A：1 家、B：6 家、D：1 家。每一行都除以这一行的期初公司数，得到原答案中的矩阵。

这里隐含采用配套答案的身份归属：9 月违约的 A 公司属于最初的 A 组。题目没有列出逐家公司身份，不能一边套原答案、一边又把这家公司改成从 B 迁来的那家。违约态 D 的最后一行由吸收态假设给定，不是把零样本当分母计算。

**（b）再算累计停留时间。**一家公司在某评级停留半年，贡献 0.5 公司年；迁移后还会给新评级贡献时间。因此：

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_A&=9\times1+2\times\frac3{12}+1\times\frac9{12}+1\times\frac6{12}=10.75,\\
N_B&=6\times1+2\times\frac6{12}+2\times\frac9{12}=8.50.
\end{aligned}
$$

$N_A$ 最后一项是 6 月由 B 迁入的公司；$N_B$ 最后一项是 3 月由 A 迁入的两家。**迁入者也要统计，违约后的时间不再贡献给 A、B。**

**（c）“次数÷出发评级的累计时间”才是强度。**A→B 共 2 次、A→D 共 1 次；B→A 和 B→D 各 1 次。例如：

$$
\widehat\lambda_{AB}=\frac2{10.75}=\frac8{43},
\qquad \widehat\lambda_{BA}=\frac1{8.5}=\frac2{17}.
$$

两者分母不同，不能因为是互相迁移就认为强度相等。生成矩阵对角项是本行离开强度之和的负数；每行和为 0，单位是年$^{-1}$。

**（d）最后把速率转成一年概率。**用矩阵指数：

$$
\widehat P_{\mathrm{gen}}(1)=e^{\widehat\Lambda}
\approx\begin{pmatrix}
0.764904&0.144393&0.090703\\
0.091307&0.798878&0.109814\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

它允许一年中发生多步迁移，所以与直接统计期末的经验矩阵不完全相同。检查标准：生成矩阵行和为 0，概率矩阵行和为 1，D 保持吸收。若题目要求手算近似，要明确写出截断阶数，如 $I+\Lambda+\Lambda^2/2$；直接给软件矩阵不能替代题目要求的展开过程。

对应课程：[[AMA572 Fixed-Income and Credit Risk/Lecture 1/01 - Lecture 1（英文原文与折叠译文）|Lecture 1]]。

### 题目

#### Question 2. Implied default probability from a bond price [10 points] <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 2 题：由债券价格推断隐含违约概率（10 分）。</span></details>

A one-year corporate bond has par value 100 and pays 104 at maturity if no default occurs. Its continuously compounded yield is 5%. The corresponding risk-free rate is 3%. Default can occur only at time 0.5, and recovery is 40 at that time. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">一只一年期公司债面值为 100，若不违约，到期支付 104。其连续复利收益率为 5%，对应无风险利率为 3%。违约只会发生在第 0.5 年，届时回收金额为 40。</span></details>

Using the expected-loss method from the lectures, compute the implied probability $p$ of default at time 0.5. In particular, compare the corporate and default-free bond prices and discount the loss given default from time 0.5 to time 0. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">使用讲义中的预期损失法（expected-loss method）计算第 0.5 年违约的隐含概率 p。具体而言，比较公司债与无违约债券的价格，并将违约损失从第 0.5 年贴现至零时刻。</span></details>

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Solution 2. Implied default probability from a bond price <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 2 题解答：由债券价格推断隐含违约概率。</span></details>

**Solution.** <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">解答。</span></details>

The corporate bond price implied by its yield is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">由收益率推得的公司债价格为：</span></details>

$$
B_{\mathrm{corp}}=104e^{-0.05}=98.9279.
$$

The otherwise identical default-free bond price is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其他条件相同的无违约债券价格为：</span></details>

$$
B_{\mathrm{rf}}=104e^{-0.03}=100.9263.
$$

Thus, the price reduction attributed to default is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，归因于违约的价格降低为：</span></details>

$$
B_{\mathrm{rf}}-B_{\mathrm{corp}}=1.9985.
$$

At time 0.5, the risk-free value of the promised payment at time 1 is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 0.5 年时，第 1 年承诺支付的无风险价值为：</span></details>

$$
104e^{-0.03(1-0.5)}=104e^{-0.015}=102.4516.
$$

If default occurs, the recovery is 40, so the loss at time 0.5 is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">若违约，回收 40，因此第 0.5 年的损失为：</span></details>

$$
102.4516-40=62.4516.
$$

Its time-zero present value is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其零时刻现值为：</span></details>

$$
62.4516e^{-0.03(0.5)}=61.5219.
$$

Equating the bond price difference to expected discounted loss gives <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">令债券价差等于预期贴现损失，得到：</span></details>

$$
1.9985=p(61.5219),
$$

and therefore <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，</span></details>

$$
p=0.03248\approx3.25\%.
$$

### 中文详细讲解

**题意与目标。**债券承诺一年后付 104，但今天市场只愿按 5% 的收益率定价。无风险利率为 3%，两种价格的差额就是本题要用违约损失解释的部分。违约只可能在半年时发生，所以只有一个未知概率 $p$。

**第一步：用各自收益率算今天的两个价格。**

$$
B_{\mathrm{corp}}=104e^{-0.05}=98.927860,
\qquad
B_{\mathrm{rf}}=104e^{-0.03}=100.926335.
$$

差额为 $1.998475$。这里的 5% 只是市场价格对应的到期收益率；后面做风险中性损失折现，使用无风险利率 3%。

**第二步：先站在违约时点，而不是今天，衡量损失。**如果半年时违约，原本一年后能拿到的 104，在半年时的无风险价值为：

$$
F(0.5)=104e^{-0.03(1-0.5)}=102.451642.
$$

此时实际拿到回收 40，所以违约发生这一刻损失的是 $102.451642-40=62.451642$，不是简单的面值损失 $100-40$。再从半年折回今天：

$$
L_0=e^{-0.03\times0.5}[102.451642-40]=61.521858.
$$

**第三步：把“条件于发生违约的损失”变成期望损失。**因为只有一个违约时点：

$$
B_{\mathrm{rf}}-B_{\mathrm{corp}}=pL_0,
\qquad
p=\frac{1.998475}{61.521858}=0.032483989.
$$

**答案：隐含半年时点违约概率约为 $3.2484\%$，可四舍五入为 $3.25\%$。**这里并非半年强度，也不是债券收益率与无风险利率之差。

可独立检查：$B_{\mathrm{corp}}=(1-p)104e^{-0.03}+p\,40e^{-0.015}$。这与上述损失方程完全相同，因为本题只有一次违约机会。

#### Question 3. Merton’s structural model [20 points] <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 3 题：Merton 结构式模型（20 分）。</span></details>

(b) Compute $d_1$, $d_2$, the equity value $S_0$, and the debt value $B_0$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）计算 d1、d2、股权价值 S0 与债务价值 B0。</span></details>

(c) Compute the risk-neutral probability of default by maturity. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（c）计算到期前的风险中性违约概率。</span></details>

(d) Compute the continuously compounded risky debt yield and the credit spread. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（d）计算风险债务的连续复利收益率和信用利差。</span></details>

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Solution 3. Merton’s structural model <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 3 题解答：Merton 结构式模型。</span></details>

**Solution.** <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">解答。</span></details>

At maturity, equity holders receive the residual value after debt is paid: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">到期时，股东获得偿债后的剩余价值：</span></details>

$$
S_T=(V_T-D)^+.
$$

This is exactly the payoff of a European call option on $V_T$ with strike $D$. Hence, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">这正是以 VT 为标的、执行价为 D 的欧式看涨期权收益。因此：</span></details>

$$
S_0=V_0\Phi(d_1)-De^{-rT}\Phi(d_2),
$$

where <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其中：</span></details>

$$
d_1=\frac{\log(V_0/D)+(r+\frac12\sigma_V^2)T}{\sigma_V\sqrt T},\quad d_2=d_1-\sigma_V\sqrt T.
$$

Numerically, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">数值上：</span></details>

$$
d_1=\frac{\log(100/80)+(0.05+0.5(0.30)^2)}{0.30}=1.06048,
$$

and <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">以及：</span></details>

$$
d_2=1.06048-0.30=0.76048.
$$

Using $\Phi(d_1)=0.85554$ and $\Phi(d_2)=0.77652$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">使用 Φ(d1)＝0.85554、Φ(d2)＝0.77652，</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}S_0&=100(0.85554)-80e^{-0.05}(0.77652)\\&=26.4621.\end{aligned}
$$

With no taxes or bankruptcy costs, debt plus equity equals firm value. Thus, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">无税收和破产成本时，债务加股权等于企业价值。因此：</span></details>

$$
B_0=V_0-S_0=100-26.4621=73.5379.
$$

Default occurs at maturity when $V_T<D$. Therefore, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">到期时若 VT＜D 则违约，因此：</span></details>

$$
P^*(V_T<D)=\Phi(-d_2)=\Phi(-0.76048)=0.22348.
$$

Thus, the risk-neutral one-year default probability is approximately 22.35%. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因而风险中性的一年期违约概率约为 22.35%。</span></details>

The continuously compounded risky debt yield $y$ satisfies <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">风险债务的连续复利收益率 y 满足：</span></details>

$$
B_0=De^{-yT}.
$$

Hence, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，</span></details>

$$
y=\frac1T\log\left(\frac D{B_0}\right)=\log\left(\frac{80}{73.5379}\right)=0.08423.
$$

The credit spread is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">信用利差为：</span></details>

$$
y-r=0.08423-0.05=0.03423\approx3.42\%,
$$

or about 342 basis points. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">即约 342 个基点。</span></details>

### 中文详细讲解

**先认模型。**这是没有票息、税收和破产成本的基本 Merton 模型，只在到期时比较 $V_T$ 与 $D$。不能在中途资产一跌破 80 时就宣布违约，那是另一个模型的机制。

**（a）为什么股权是看涨期权？**到期资产超过 80，先还债，股东拿剩余；不够还债，股东最多损失股权投资，收益为零。因此 $S_T=(V_T-80)^+$，与执行价为 80 的欧式看涨期权一致。

**（b）先算 $d_1,d_2$，再代入价格。**本题 $\sigma_V\sqrt T=0.3$，所以：

$$
d_1=\frac{\ln(100/80)+(0.05+0.3^2/2)}{0.3}=1.0604785,
\qquad d_2=0.7604785.
$$

接着用看涨期权式：

$$
S_0=100\Phi(d_1)-80e^{-0.05}\Phi(d_2)=26.4620857.
$$

因没有额外税盾与破产损失，债股只是分同一份资产，故：

$$
B_0=V_0-S_0=73.5379143.
$$

这里的 $B_0$ 是债的市场价格，80 是到期承诺面值，两者含义不同。

**（c）违约概率为什么用 $d_2$？**资产的对数增量满足：

$$
\ln(V_T/V_0)=(r-\tfrac12\sigma_V^2)T+\sigma_V\sqrt T Z,
\qquad Z\sim N(0,1).
$$

把 $V_T<D$ 标准化后，事件恰好是 $Z<-d_2$，所以：

$$
P^*(V_T<80)=\Phi(-0.7604785)=0.2234843\approx22.3484\%.
$$

$\Phi(d_1)$ 是期权 Delta 中的量，不能看它出现次数多就把它当成存活或违约概率。

**（d）零息债才可直接从面值和价格反求收益率。**

$$
y=\ln\frac{80}{73.5379143}=0.08422552,
\qquad y-r=0.03422552.
$$

**最终：收益率约 $8.4226\%$，信用利差约 $3.4226\%$，即 $342.26$ bp/年。**一个基点等于 $0.01\%$，因此将小数形式的利差乘 10,000 才得到 bp。核验债价小于无风险承诺现值 $80e^{-0.05}$，对应正利差。

### 题目

#### Question 4. First passage time and the Black-Cox model [15 points] <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 4 题：首次穿越时间与 Black–Cox 模型（15 分）。</span></details>

Under the risk-neutral measure, let <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">在风险中性测度下，设：</span></details>

$$
\frac{dV_t}{V_t}=(r-\delta)\,dt+\sigma\,dW_t,\qquad\tau=\inf\{t\geq0:V_t\leq V_B\},
$$

where $0<V_B<V_0$. Define <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">其中 0＜VB＜V0。定义：</span></details>

$$
\mu=r-\delta-\frac12\sigma^2,\qquad b=\log\left(\frac{V_B}{V_0}\right)<0.
$$

(a) State the Laplace transform $E[e^{-s\tau}]$ for $s>0$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）写出 s＞0 时的拉普拉斯变换 E[e^(−sτ)]。</span></details>

(b) Show that the probability of default by time $T$ is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（b）证明截至 T 时刻的违约概率为：</span></details>

$$
P(\tau\leq T)=\Phi\left(\frac{b-\mu T}{\sigma\sqrt T}\right)+\exp\left(\frac{2\mu b}{\sigma^2}\right)\Phi\left(\frac{b+\mu T}{\sigma\sqrt T}\right).
$$

(c) Evaluate this probability when $V_0=100$, $V_B=70$, $r=4\%$, $\delta=2\%$, $\sigma=25\%$, and $T=2$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（c）当 V0＝100、VB＝70、r＝4%、δ＝2%、σ＝25%、T＝2 时，计算上述概率。</span></details>

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Solution 4. First passage time and the Black-Cox model <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 4 题解答：首次穿越时间与 Black–Cox 模型。</span></details>

**Solution.** <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">解答。</span></details>

Taking logarithms of the asset process gives <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对资产过程取对数，得到：</span></details>

$$
\log\left(\frac{V_t}{V_0}\right)=\mu t+\sigma W_t.
$$

Thus, $\tau$ is the first time a Brownian motion with drift $\mu$ and volatility $\sigma$ hits the lower level $b<0$. Its Laplace transform is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因而 τ 是漂移 μ、波动率 σ 的布朗运动首次触及下方水平 b＜0 的时刻。其拉普拉斯变换为：</span></details>

$$
E[e^{-s\tau}]=\exp\left\{\frac b{\sigma^2}(\sqrt{\mu^2+2\sigma^2s}+\mu)\right\},\quad s>0.
$$

The reflection principle for Brownian motion with drift gives the probability that the running minimum crosses $b$ by time $T$: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">带漂移布朗运动的反射原理（reflection principle）给出截至 T 时刻运行最小值穿过 b 的概率：</span></details>

$$
P(\tau\leq T)=\Phi\left(\frac{b-\mu T}{\sigma\sqrt T}\right)+\exp\left(\frac{2\mu b}{\sigma^2}\right)\Phi\left(\frac{b+\mu T}{\sigma\sqrt T}\right).
$$

The two terms correspond to paths ending below the barrier and paths ending above the barrier after having crossed it earlier. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">两项分别对应期末位于边界下方的路径，以及之前曾穿过边界、但期末回到边界上方的路径。</span></details>

For the stated parameters, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对题给参数，</span></details>

$$
\mu=0.04-0.02-\frac12(0.25)^2=-0.01125,\quad b=\log(0.70)=-0.356675.
$$

Also, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">此外，</span></details>

$$
\frac{b-\mu T}{\sigma\sqrt T}=-0.94519,\quad\frac{b+\mu T}{\sigma\sqrt T}=-1.07247,
$$

and <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">以及：</span></details>

$$
\exp\left(\frac{2\mu b}{\sigma^2}\right)=1.13701.
$$

Therefore, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}P(\tau\leq2)&=\Phi(-0.94519)+1.13701\Phi(-1.07247)\\&=0.17228+1.13701(0.14175)\\&=0.33346.\end{aligned}
$$

Thus, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因而：</span></details>

$$
P(\tau\leq2)\approx33.35\%.
$$

### 中文详细讲解

**本题的目标已换了。**它问的是“截至 $T$，资产路径有没有曾经碰过下限”，而不是只看 $V_T$ 最后落在哪里。Lecture 3 的首次穿越工具正是为此服务。

**（a）先把资产触界改写成布朗运动触界。**对资产取对数后：

$$
\ln(V_t/V_0)=\mu t+\sigma W_t,
\qquad \mu=r-\delta-\frac12\sigma^2.
$$

资产下限 $V_B$ 对应的对数边界 $b=\ln(V_B/V_0)<0$。因此使用**下穿**公式，根号外是“加 $\mu$”：

$$
E^*[e^{-s\tau}]
=\exp\left[\frac{b}{\sigma^2}
\left(\sqrt{\mu^2+2\sigma^2s}+\mu\right)\right].
$$

$\tau=\infty$ 时取指数为 0。$s$ 是 Laplace 参数，不能把它看成违约强度。

**（b）怎样理解违约概率的两项？**带漂移布朗运动的反射结果把触界路径分为两类：到期时仍在边界下方的路径，以及曾碰线但后来反弹、到期在边界上方的路径。两部分加起来才是首次穿越概率：

$$
P^*(\tau\leq T)
=\Phi\left(\frac{b-\mu T}{\sigma\sqrt T}\right)
+e^{2\mu b/\sigma^2}
\Phi\left(\frac{b+\mu T}{\sigma\sqrt T}\right).
$$

若只保留第一项，就只统计最终落在下方的路径，漏掉了“已经违约、后来反弹”的路径。

若要把题目的“Show that”写成可核验的步骤，还可以从 Lecture 3 的下穿时间密度验证。对 $b<0$：

$$
f_\tau(u)=\frac{-b}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi u^3}}
\exp\left[-\frac{(b-\mu u)^2}{2\sigma^2u}\right],\qquad u>0.
$$

将目标式右边记作 $F(T)$，对 $T$ 求导可得 $F'(T)=f_\tau(T)$，同时 $F(T)$ 在 $T\downarrow0$ 时趋于 0，所以 $F(T)=\int_0^T f_\tau(u)du=P(\tau\leq T)$。这给出了结果的验证路线，而不是把终值概率误当成触界概率。

**（c）按顺序代入，避免符号混乱。**

$$
\mu=0.04-0.02-\tfrac12(0.25)^2=-0.01125,
\qquad b=\ln(0.7)=-0.35667494.
$$

两项正态变量与修正系数分别为：

$$
z_1=-0.94518948,
\qquad z_2=-1.07246870,
\qquad e^{2\mu b/\sigma^2}=1.13701110.
$$

于是：

$$
P^*(\tau\leq2)=\Phi(z_1)+1.13701110\Phi(z_2)
=0.33345784\approx33.3458\%.
$$

**易错点：**$b$ 为负，$\mu$ 也可能为负；指数修正系数可以大于 1，但两个项的和才是概率。$\delta$ 在这里是资产支付率，不是回收率。

### 题目

In the Leland-Toft model, new debt with par value $p\,dt$ is issued continuously. Each bond has an exponentially distributed maturity with density $\phi(u)=me^{-mu}$, so the mean maturity is $1/m$. (a) Show that the stationary total par value of outstanding debt is $P=p/m$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（a）证明未偿债务的稳态总面值为 P＝p／m。</span></details>

(c) Let <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">（c）令：</span></details>

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Solution 5. Leland-Toft endogenous default <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 5 题解答：Leland–Toft 内生违约。</span></details>

**Solution.** <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">解答。</span></details>

At time $t$, debt issued at time $u<t$ has remaining maturity $s-t$ when its original maturity is $s-u$. The par value maturing in $(s,s+ds)$ is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">在 t 时刻，若 u＜t 时发行债务的原始期限为 s−u，则剩余期限为 s−t。在 (s，s＋ds) 到期的债务面值为：</span></details>

$$
\left(\int_{-\infty}^t pme^{-m(s-u)}\,du\right)ds=pe^{-m(s-t)}\,ds.
$$

Integrating over all future maturity dates gives <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对所有未来到期日期积分，得到：</span></details>

$$
P=\int_t^\infty pe^{-m(s-t)}\,ds=\frac pm.
$$

At default, the aggregate present value of the individual bond recoveries is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">违约时，各债券回收支付的总现值为：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_\tau^\infty ce^{-r(s-\tau)}pe^{-m(s-\tau)}\,ds&=cp\int_0^\infty e^{-(m+r)u}\,du\\&=\frac{cp}{m+r}=\frac{cmP}{m+r},
\end{aligned}
$$

where $p=mP$ was used. Matching this amount to $\alpha V_B$ yields <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">这里使用了 p＝mP。令此金额等于 αVB，得到：</span></details>

$$
\frac{cmP}{m+r}=\alpha V_B,\quad c=\frac{m+r}m\frac{\alpha V_B}P.
$$

The Modigliani-Miller decomposition in this setting is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">该设定下的 Modigliani–Miller 分解为：</span></details>

$$
v(V;V_B)=V+E\left[\int_0^\tau\kappa\rho Pe^{-rt}\,dt\right]-(1-\alpha)V_BE[e^{-r\tau}].
$$

Since <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">由于：</span></details>

$$
\int_0^\tau e^{-rt}\,dt=\frac{1-e^{-r\tau}}r,
$$

we obtain <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">得到：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
v(V;V_B)&=V+\frac{\kappa\rho P}r(1-E[e^{-r\tau}])-(1-\alpha)V_BE[e^{-r\tau}]\\
&=V+\frac{\kappa\rho}rP-\left[\frac{\kappa\rho}rP+(1-\alpha)V_B\right]E[e^{-r\tau}].
\end{aligned}
$$

Using <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">使用：</span></details>

$$
E[e^{-r\tau}]=\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^{\gamma_r},\quad\gamma_r=\sqrt{\xi^2+\frac{2r}{\sigma^2}}+\xi,
$$

gives <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">得到：</span></details>

$$
v(V;V_B)=V+\frac{\kappa\rho}rP-\left[\frac{\kappa\rho}rP+(1-\alpha)V_B\right]\left(\frac{V_B}V\right)^{\gamma_r}.
$$

The optimization has two stages because debt and equity holders have different objectives. For a fixed debt level $P$, equity holders choose the default boundary $V_B$ to maximize equity value subject to limited liability. The value-matching and smooth-pasting conditions at the boundary lead to a boundary of the form $V_B^*(P)=\epsilon P$. At the second stage, the firm anticipates this equity-holder decision and chooses $P$ to maximize $v(V;V_B^*(P))$. The optimum balances the tax advantage of debt against expected bankruptcy costs. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">优化分为两阶段，是因为债权人与股东目标不同。给定债务水平 P，股东在有限责任约束下选择 VB 以最大化股权价值。边界处的价值匹配（value matching）与光滑粘贴（smooth pasting）条件导出 VB*(P)＝εP。第二阶段，企业预见股东的决策，选择 P 以最大化 v(V；VB*(P))，在债务税收优势与预期破产成本之间取得平衡。</span></details>

### 中文详细讲解

**四小问是一条链：**先描述所有债券何时到期，再匹配回收，接着算企业总价值，最后解释怎样决定违约和融资。这对应 Lecture 4。

**（a）为什么总面值是 $p/m$？**$p$ 是每年的发行面值速度；指数期限参数 $m$ 控制到期速度，平均期限为 $1/m$。在平稳状态下，未来还有 $u$ 年到期的存续面值密度为 $pe^{-mu}$，因此：

$$
P=\int_0^\infty pe^{-mu}\,du=\frac{p}{m}.
$$

这不是单只债的市场价格，而是所有未到期债务的面值总和。

**（b）为什么 $c$ 不直接等于 $\alpha$？**$\alpha$ 是全体债权人收回公司资产的比例；$c$ 则乘在单只债券对应的无风险零息债价值上，基准不同。违约时把所有债券的回收加总：

$$
\int_0^\infty ce^{-ru}pe^{-mu}\,du
=\frac{cp}{m+r}=\frac{cmP}{m+r}.
$$

连续资产触界时 $V_\tau=V_B$。把总回收等于 $\alpha V_B$，解出：

$$
c=\frac{m+r}{m}\frac{\alpha V_B}{P}.
$$

$V_B$ 随机触达的时刻是随机的，但触界时的资产水平等于给定边界，所以可以把 $V_B$ 提出期望。

**（c）把企业价值拆成三项。**本题最重要的经济结构是：

$$
\text{企业总价值}=V+\text{违约前税盾现值}-\text{违约时破产损失现值}.
$$

每年税盾速度为 $\kappa\rho P$，只持续到违约；每条路径上票息税盾积分为 $\kappa\rho P(1-e^{-r\tau})/r$。令：

$$
H_r=E^*[e^{-r\tau}]=(V_B/V)^{\gamma_r}.
$$

因此先得到 $v=V+(\kappa\rho P/r)(1-H_r)-(1-\alpha)V_BH_r$；整理后就是题目要求的：

$$
v(V;V_B)=V+\frac{\kappa\rho P}{r}
-\left[\frac{\kappa\rho P}{r}+(1-\alpha)V_B\right](V_B/V)^{\gamma_r}.
$$

这一步把 Lecture 2 的“税盾与破产损失权衡”和 Lecture 3 的“首次穿越时间折现期望”接了起来。

**（d）为什么分两阶段？**发债以后，股东控制公司的违约决定，所以先固定面值 $P$，求使股权 $S$ 最大、且违约前股权不为负的边界 $V_B^*(P)$。再在今天发行债务时，预见股东将来的行为，选择使企业总价值 $v(V;V_B^*(P))$ 最大的 $P$。前一步优化股权，后一步优化企业价值；股东和债主的利益不完全一致，所以不能随意把两个问题合成一个。

**易错点：**$P$ 是面值，$D(V;V_B)$ 是总债市场价值；$p$ 是发行速度。最优边界与债务规模有关，并非外生给定的 Black–Cox 边界。

### 答案（原 PDF）

#### Solution 6. Duffie-Singleton reduced-form pricing <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 6 题解答：Duffie–Singleton 简约式定价。</span></details>

**Solution.** <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">解答。</span></details>

Over a short interval $dt$, default occurs with probability approximately $\lambda\,dt$. If default occurs, the holder keeps the fraction $R_M$ of market value; otherwise, the full continuation value is retained. Ignoring terms of order $(dt)^2$, the one-step discount multiplier is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">短区间 dt 内，违约概率约为 λdt。若违约，持有人保留市场价值的比例 RM；否则保留全部延续价值。忽略 (dt)² 阶项，单步贴现乘数为：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
e^{-r\,dt}[\lambda\,dt\,R_M+(1-\lambda\,dt)]&\approx(1-r\,dt)[1-(1-R_M)\lambda\,dt]\\&\approx1-[r+(1-R_M)\lambda]\,dt.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus, the effective discount rate is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因而有效贴现率为：</span></details>

$$
r+(1-R_M)\lambda.
$$

For a zero-coupon bond with unit face value, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对单位面值零息债券，</span></details>

$$
B(0,T)=\exp(-[r+(1-R_M)\lambda]T).
$$

With $T=4$, $r=0.03$, $\lambda=0.02$, and $R_M=0.40$, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">代入 T＝4、r＝0.03、λ＝0.02、RM＝0.40，</span></details>

$$
r+(1-R_M)\lambda=0.03+0.60(0.02)=0.042.
$$

Therefore, <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">因此，</span></details>

$$
B(0,4)=e^{-0.042(4)}=e^{-0.168}=0.84535.
$$

The continuously compounded yield is 4.20%, and the credit spread is <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">连续复利收益率为 4.20%，信用利差为：</span></details>

$$
4.20\%-3.00\%=1.20\%=120\text{ basis points}.
$$

### 中文详细讲解

**先看回收基准。**本题在违约时保留的是“违约前市场价值”的 $R_M$，不是面值的 $R_M$。这是 Duffie–Singleton 公式能简化为调整折现率的关键条件。

**（a）短时间内分两种情况。**接下来的 $dt$ 内不违约的概率约为 $1-\lambda dt$，保留全部价值；违约概率约为 $\lambda dt$，只保留比例 $R_M$。合并后的风险调整乘数是：

$$
(1-\lambda dt)+R_M\lambda dt
=1-(1-R_M)\lambda dt.
$$

再乘正常无风险折现 $e^{-r dt}$，保留一阶项，得到：

$$
1-[r+(1-R_M)\lambda]dt.
$$

所以有效折现率是 $r+(1-R_M)\lambda$。直觉上，“出事速度×出事损失比例”是信用折现调整。

**（b）常参数零息债直接折现。**

$$
r_{\mathrm{eff}}=0.03+(1-0.4)\times0.02=0.042,
$$

$$
B(0,4)=e^{-0.042\times4}=0.84535383.
$$

**答案：单位面值债价约 $0.84535$，收益率 $4.20\%$，信用利差 $1.20\%=120$ bp/年。**如果面值为 100，价格同比例乘 100。

注意 $\lambda=2\%$ 是连续时间强度，一年违约概率为 $1-e^{-0.02}$，不严格等于 2%。本题债价也不是把 $e^{-rT}$ 乘上一个简单面值回收概率公式；那会切换到不同的回收假设。

s\approx1.6784\%=167.84\text{ basis points per year}.
$$

### 中文详细讲解

**这题在比较两方付的钱。**买方支付常规保费和违约时的应计保费；卖方支付违约损失补偿。初始公平的 CDS 令两边的预期现值相等。$q=2.5\%$ 明确是每年的条件违约概率，因此这题必须乘此前存活概率。

**第一步：为每一年列两种互斥事件。**存活到第 $k$ 年末的概率为：

$$
S_k=(1-q)^k.
$$

第 $k$ 年中违约，需要先活过 $k-1$ 年，再在本年违约，所以：

$$
Q_k=(1-q)^{k-1}q.
$$

例如第三年中违约的无条件概率是 $0.975^2\times0.025=0.023765625$，而不是每一行都写 0.025。

**第二步：同一年对应三笔现金流。**常规保费在年末 $k$ 支付，乘 $S_k$；年中违约时只补半年保费，支付时间是 $k-0.5$，乘 $Q_k/2$；保护赔付同在年中，金额是 $1-R=0.65$，乘 $Q_k$。

按单位名义本金，三部分现值分别是：

$$
\begin{aligned}
PV_{\mathrm{regular}}&=s\sum_{k=1}^5S_ke^{-0.04k},\\
PV_{\mathrm{accrual}}&=\frac{s}{2}\sum_{k=1}^5Q_ke^{-0.04(k-1/2)},\\
PV_{\mathrm{protection}}&=0.65\sum_{k=1}^5Q_ke^{-0.04(k-1/2)}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**第三步：先加总系数，再解 $s$。**复算结果为：

$$
A=4.127809865,
\qquad B=0.053989707,
\qquad C=0.070186619.
$$

$A,B$ 是需乘保费率 $s$ 的系数，$C$ 是保护腿现值。因此：

$$
s(A+B)=C,
\qquad
s=0.0167838315=1.67838315\%=167.8383\text{ bp/年}.
$$

**答案约为 $167.84$ bp/年。**原答案表中有小数取整，最后若有极小差别，保留中间精度即可。

**检查与易错。**所有年度违约概率之和，加第五年末存活概率，必须等于 1；违约当年不能再交完整年末保费，只补约定的半年。若漏掉应计保费，分母从 $A+B$ 变成 $A$，解出的保费率反而偏高。名义本金在公平保费的两边同时出现并抵消，不需要把“单位本金”再额外当作金额折现。
