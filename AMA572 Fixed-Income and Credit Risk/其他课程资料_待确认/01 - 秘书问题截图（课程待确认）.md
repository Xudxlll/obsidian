## 印刷原文还原（`final534-sol-1` · Question 5）

**Question 5** *(Marks: 5 + 10)* <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">第 5 题（分值栏标注：前段 5 分，末尾 10 分）。</span></details>

Interview the first $r$ applicants, but do not select any of them. Starting from applicant $r+1$, select the first applicant who is better than all previous applicants. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">先面试前 r 位候选人，但一个也不录用。从第 r+1 位候选人开始，录用第一位比此前所有候选人都更优秀的候选人。</span></details>

Let the best applicant be in position $j$ ($1 \le j \le n$). <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">设最优秀的候选人位于第 j 个位置（1 ≤ j ≤ n）。</span></details>

The best applicant can be in any position with equal probability: $\mathbb{P}(\text{the applicant selected}) = \frac{1}{n}$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">最优秀的候选人等可能地出现在任意位置：P(the applicant selected) = 1/n。（注：原文事件名写作 "the applicant selected"，语义上本意应为 "best is at position j"，照录。）</span></details>

We can only select the best applicant if they appear after the first $r$ applicants: $j > r$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">只有当最优秀的候选人出现在前 r 位之后（即 j &gt; r）时，我们才有可能选中他／她。</span></details>

When the best applicant appears at position $j$, we will select them if and only if no earlier applicant (positions $r+1$ to $j-1$) is better than all previous ones (i.e., the best among the first $j-1$ applicants is among the first $r$ applicants). <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">当最优秀的候选人出现在第 j 位时，我们会选中他／她，当且仅当此前（第 r+1 位到第 j−1 位）没有候选人比其前面所有人都更优秀（即前 j−1 位中的最优秀者落在前 r 位之中）。</span></details>

The probability that the best among the first $j-1$ applicants is in the first $r$ positions is $\mathbb{P}(\text{record in } \{1, 2, \dots, r\} \mid j) = \frac{r}{j-1}$. *(右侧 Marks 栏：`5`)* <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">前 j−1 位中的最优秀者落在前 r 个位置的概率为 P(record in {1, 2, …, r} | j) = r / (j−1)。（右侧 Marks 栏标注：5。）</span></details>

Sum over all possible positions $j$ where $j > r$: <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">对所有满足 j &gt; r 的可能位置 j 求和：</span></details>

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(r) &= \sum_{j=r+1}^{n} \mathbb{P}(\text{best is at position } j, \text{the applicant selected}) \\
&= \sum_{j=r+1}^{n} \mathbb{P}(\text{record in } \{1, 2, \dots, r\} \mid j) \times \mathbb{P}(\text{the applicant selected}) \\
&= \sum_{j=r+1}^{n} \frac{r}{j-1} \cdot \frac{1}{n} = \frac{r}{n} \sum_{j=r+1}^{n} \frac{1}{j-1} \\
&\approx \frac{r}{n} \int_{r}^{n} \frac{1}{x}\, dx = \frac{r}{n}(\ln n - \ln r) = \frac{r}{n} \ln\left(\frac{n}{r}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

To estimate the best probability, we consider $\max_{r} f(r)$. Taking the first order for $f(r)$ with respect to $r$ yields <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">为估计最大成功概率，考虑 max_r f(r)。对 f(r) 关于 r 求一阶导数，得（注：原文 "first order" 漏写 "derivative"，照录）：</span></details>

$$
f'(r) = \frac{1}{n} \ln\left(\frac{n}{r}\right) - \frac{1}{n}.
$$

Setting $f'(r) = 0$ leads to $r = \frac{n}{e}$. <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">令 f'(r) = 0 可得 r = n / e。</span></details>

$$
f(r) \approx \frac{n/e}{n} \ln\left(\frac{n}{n/e}\right) = \frac{1}{e} \ln(e) = \frac{1}{e} \cdot 1 = \frac{1}{e}.
$$

Since $r = \frac{n}{e} \approx \frac{3000}{e} = 1105.8$, we choose the optimal $r = 1106$. *(右侧 Marks 栏：`10`)* <details class="translation-details"><summary>中文译文</summary><span class="translation-body">由于 r = n/e ≈ 3000/e = 1105.8，我们取最优阈值 r = 1106。（右侧 Marks 栏标注：10。注：原 PDF 印刷确为 1105.8 与 1106，实际 3000/e ≈ 1103.64，此处照录原文并注明疑点。）</span></details>
