# Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) Architecture

01 MLP with 4 hidden layers of 400 neurons each achieves 94.5% MSE reduction versus Black-Scholes. Leaky ReLU activation and batch normalization enable stable training for deep OTM options where traditional models fail.

# LSTM Networks for Volatility Dynamics

02 LSTM learns volatility patterns directly from 20-day historical windows without explicit sigma inputs. Superior performance for options exceeding 3 months maturity, capturing temporal dependencies invisible to parametric models.

# CNN-LSTM Hybrid & Key Advantages

03 Hybrid architecture captures local order book features plus sequential dependencies. Eliminates parametric assumptions, enables multi-task bid-ask prediction, and adapts continuously to evolving market microstructure.

# LSTM-HIT: Hybrid Volatility Forecasting Architecture

# 01

# Three-Component Integration Framework

LSTM-HIT combines High-Frequency Realized Volatility from 5-15 minute intervals, Technical Indicators (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands), and Traditional Model parameters from GARCH/HAR frameworks. This comprehensive architecture addresses critical limitations of conventional models by simultaneously capturing long-term memory, nonlinear high-frequency dynamics, and technical regime signals.

# LSTM-HIT-EVT for Risk Management

Extended model integrates Extreme Value Theory using semiparametric methods for standardized return quantile estimation. Delivers superior Value-at-Risk forecasts with best-in-class accuracy for tail risk quantification, outperforming traditional historical simulation and variance-covariance approaches in regulatory stress testing scenarios.

# Geopolitical Energy Shocks Analysis

# Iraq War vs Russia-Ukraine War: Comparative Impact

# Iraq War 2003: Temporary Shock Characteristics

Price movement from \$25 to \$37 (+40%) over 8-week spike followed by stabilization. Minimal supply disruption with preserved infrastructure. Prices normalized within months as risk premium unwound once uncertainty cleared. Mean-reverting market structure.

# Russia-Ukraine War: Permanent

Regime Shift
Sustained elevation from \$78 to \$120+ with continued volatility. 11.5% of global exports affected triggering European energy crisis. ARFIMA analysis confirms permanent shock with d=0.52, indicating I(0) to I(1) regime shift and structural restructuring of global energy flows.

Key Diffors scope: Limited military-focused versus comprehensive financial warfare including SWIFT exclusion. Supply chain: quick restoration versus massive European restructuring and LNG pivot. Energy transition: no acceleration versus REPowerEU catalyst. Market structure: mean-reverting versus permanent shift.
