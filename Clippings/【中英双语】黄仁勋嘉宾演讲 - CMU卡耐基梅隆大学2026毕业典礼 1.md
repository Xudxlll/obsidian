## 演讲总结

黄仁勋在 CMU 2026 年毕业典礼上的演讲，以**个人经历、创业教训、AI 时代展望**三条主线交织，核心传递了以下信息：

1. **感恩与谦逊**：开场致敬母亲和家庭，将自己定位为"第一代移民的美国梦实现者"。他强调成功从来不是一个人的事，背后是无数人的托举。

2. **失败是韧性之源**：通过 NVIDIA 濒死经历（技术失败、世嘉救命钱），他传达了一个核心认知——**失败不是成功的对立面，而是学习、谦逊和品格磨砺的时刻**。CEO 也不是权力，而是让公司活下去的责任。

3. **AI 是不可逆的范式转换**：计算正在经历 60 年来最彻底的重新设定——从"人类编程、机器执行"切换到"机器学习和推理"。这个变革比 PC、互联网、移动、云计算任何一次都更大。

4. **不恐惧，去参与**：面对"AI 会不会取代工作"的焦虑，他的回答是——**AI 不会取代你，但更会用 AI 的人可能取代你**。历史证明，逃避技术的社会不会阻止进步，只会丧失塑造进步的权利。

5. **人人都是程序员**：AI 最大的意义在于弥合技术鸿沟，让不会写代码的人也能创造价值。机会不应只属于少数人。

6. **跑起来，别走**：以 CMU 校训"My heart is in the work"收束全篇，号召毕业生以乐观、责任和雄心去拥抱 AI 时代，打造值得自己教育、潜能和所有相信你的人的东西。

> 一句话概括：**用乐观和责任感参与 AI 革命，而不是恐惧它。**

## 演讲全文（中英对照整理）

### 开场与致谢

> Thank you for your many contributions to advancing science and technology... And now please join me in extending a warm welcome to Jensen Huang, our keynote speaker.
>
> 感谢你们为推动科学和技术进步所做的诸多贡献……现在请和我一起热烈欢迎 Jensen Huang，我们的主旨演讲嘉宾。

> President Jahanian, members of the Board of Trustees, faculty, distinguished guests, proud parents and families, and above all, the Carnegie Mellon CLASS OF 2026.
>
> Jahanian 校长、董事会成员、教授们、尊贵的来宾、骄傲的父母和家庭，以及最重要的——卡内基梅隆大学 2026 届毕业生们。

> Today is a day of pride and joy. A dream come true for you. But not only for you. Your families, teachers, mentors, and friends helped carry you here. Before we talk about the future, thank them. This day belongs to them, too.
>
> 今天是骄傲与喜悦的一天，是你们梦想成真的一天。但不仅是你们的。你们的家人、老师、导师和朋友帮助你们走到这里。在谈论未来之前，先感谢他们。这一天也属于他们。

> Graduates, please stand up... Especially, turn to your mothers and wish them a happy Mother's Day. For you, this is another step in your life. But for her, this is a dream come true.
>
> 毕业生们，请起立……转向你们的母亲，祝她们母亲节快乐。对你们来说，这只是人生中的又一步。但对母亲来说，这是梦想成真。

### 移民家庭的美国梦

> To see you graduate from one of the world's great institutions, this is her moment, too. My mom and dad are deeply proud of me as well. My journey is their journey. I am their dream come true. And their dream was the American dream. Like many in this audience, I'm a first-generation immigrant.
>
> 看着你们从世界上最好的学府之一毕业，这也是她的时刻。我的父母也为我深感自豪。我的旅程就是他们的旅程，我是他们梦想的实现。而他们的梦想，就是美国梦。和在场许多人一样，我是第一代移民。

> My father had a dream to raise his family in America. When I was 9 years old, he sent my older brother and me to the United States. We ended up at a Baptist boarding school in Oneida, Kentucky. Coal country. A town of a few hundred people.
>
> 我父亲梦想在美国家庭团聚。我 9 岁时，他把我和哥哥送到了美国。我们最终落脚在肯塔基州奥奈达镇的一所浸信会寄宿学校——产煤区，一个只有几百人的小镇。

> My mother worked as a maid at a Catholic school. She woke me up at 4:00 a.m. in the morning to deliver newspapers. My older brother got me a job as a dishwasher at Denny's, which at the time felt like a major career advancement.
>
> 我的母亲在一所天主教学校做女佣。她凌晨 4 点就把我叫醒去送报纸。我哥哥帮我在 Denny's 找了份洗碗工的工作——当时这感觉像是重大的职业晋升。

> That was my view of America. Not easy, but full of opportunities. Not a guarantee, but a chance. My parents came here because they believed America could give their children a chance.
>
> 这就是我眼中的美国——不容易，但充满机会。不是保障，而是机会。我父母来到这里，是因为他们相信美国能给他们的孩子一个机会。

> **How can we not be romantic about America?**
>
> **我们怎能不对美国怀有浪漫呢？**

### 大学、爱情与家庭

> I went to Oregon State University. I met my wife, Lori, when I was 17 years old. I was the youngest kid in school. We were sophomore lab partners. She was 19. An older woman. I beat out 250 other boys in class and won her heart. We've now been married for 40 years.
>
> 我去了俄勒冈州立大学。17 岁时，我遇到了我的妻子 Lori。那时我是全校最小的学生，我们是大二的实验课搭档。她 19 岁——一位"年长的女士"。我打败了班上另外 250 个男生，赢得了她的心。我们结婚已经 40 年了。

### NVIDIA 的诞生与至暗时刻

> When I was 30, I started Nvidia with Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem, two amazing computer scientists. We wanted to build a new kind of computer, one that could solve problems ordinary computers could not. We had absolutely no idea how to build a company, raise money, or run Nvidia.
>
> 我 30 岁时，和两位出色的计算机科学家 Chris Malachowsky 和 Curtis Priem 一起创办了 NVIDIA。我们想打造一种新型计算机，能够解决普通计算机无法解决的问题。但我们完全不知道如何去建立一家公司、如何融资、如何运营 NVIDIA。

> **I just thought, "How hard could it be?"** ... It turns out it is super hard.
>
> **我当时只是想："能有多难？"** ……事实证明，真的超级难。

> Our first technology didn't even work. We nearly ran out of money. At one point, I had to fly to Japan and explain to Sega's CEO that the technology they contracted us to build would not work. Asked to be released from a contract we could not complete. And then asked that they still pay us. Without the money Nvidia would vaporize.
>
> 我们的第一项技术甚至没法用。我们差点烧光所有钱。有一次，我不得不飞到日本，向世嘉 CEO 解释我们签合同要做的技术根本做不出来，请求解除这份无法完成的合同——然后还请求他们照样付钱。没有那笔钱，NVIDIA 就会灰飞烟灭。

> It was embarrassing, humiliating, and one of the hardest things I have ever done. And Sega CEO Irimajiri-san said yes.
>
> 那令人尴尬、丢脸，是我做过的最艰难的事之一。而世嘉 CEO 入交先生答应了。

> **I learned early that being CEO is not about power, but the responsibility that comes with keeping the company alive. And that honesty and humility can be met with generosity and kindness, even in business.**
>
> **我很早就学到：做 CEO 不是关于权力，而是维系公司生存的责任。诚实与谦逊，即使在商业中，也能换来慷慨与善意。**

> We used the money to reset the company, and out of desperation we invented new ways of designing chips and computers that we still use today.
>
> 我们用那笔钱重启了公司，在绝望中发明了至今仍在使用的芯片和计算机设计新方法。

### 关于失败与韧性

> **We learned never to see failure as the opposite of success. Each failure is just another learning moment, a humility moment, a character-strengthening moment. The resilience forged through setbacks is what gives you the strength to go again.**
>
> **我们学会了永远不要把失败看作成功的对立面。每一次失败只是又一个学习的时刻、谦逊的时刻、磨砺品格的时刻。挫折锻造的韧性，才是让你有力量重新出发的东西。**

> Today, I am one of the longest-serving CEOs in technology. Nvidia, the body of work I share with 45,000 extraordinary colleagues, is my life's work.
>
> 今天，我是科技行业任职最长的 CEO 之一。NVIDIA——我与 45,000 位非凡同事共同创造的作品——是我一生的事业。

### AI 革命：CMU 的遗产

> Now, it's your time to realize your dreams. And the timing could not be more perfect. My career started at the beginning of the PC revolution. Your career starts at the beginning of the AI revolution. I cannot imagine a more exciting time to work, to begin your life's work.
>
> 现在，轮到你们去实现梦想了。时机再好不过了。我的职业生涯始于 PC 革命的开端，而你们的职业生涯始于 AI 革命的开端。我无法想象还有比这更激动人心的时代去工作、去开始你们一生的事业。

> **AI started right here at Carnegie Mellon.** Carnegie Mellon is one of the true birthplaces of artificial intelligence and robotics. In the 1950s, researchers here created the Logic Theorist, widely recognized as the first AI computer program. In 1979, Carnegie Mellon founded the Robotics Institute, the first academic institute devoted entirely to robotics.
>
> **AI 就从这里——卡内基梅隆大学——起步。** CMU 是人工智能和机器人学真正的发源地之一。1950 年代，这里的研究者创造了"逻辑理论家"程序，被广泛认为是第一个 AI 计算机程序。1979 年，CMU 成立了机器人研究所，这是全球第一个专门致力于机器人学的研究机构。

### 计算范式的彻底重置

> I have lived through every major computing platform shift. Mainframes, PCs, the internet, mobile, and cloud. Each wave built on the one before. Each expanded access, each transformed industries and society, but what is about to happen now is bigger than anything before. **Computing is undergoing a complete reset.**
>
> 我经历过每一次重大的计算平台变革——大型机、PC、互联网、移动和云。每一波浪潮都建立在之前的基础上，每一次都扩大了使用范围，每一次都改变了行业和社会。但即将发生的事，比之前任何一次都更大。**计算正在经历彻底的重新设定。**

> For 60 years, computing worked the same way. Humans wrote software, computers executed instructions. **That paradigm is over.** Artificial intelligence has reinvented computing. From human coding to machine learning. From software running on CPUs to neural networks running on GPUs. And from following instructions to understanding, reasoning, planning, and using tools.
>
> 60 年来，计算以同样的方式运行：人类写软件，计算机执行指令。**这个范式终结了。** 人工智能彻底重塑了计算——从人类编码到机器学习，从 CPU 上运行软件到 GPU 上运行神经网络，从执行指令到理解、推理、规划和运用工具。

> A new industry has emerged to manufacture intelligence at scale. Because intelligence is foundational to every industry, every industry will change.
>
> 一个全新的产业已经诞生：大规模制造智能。因为智能是每个行业的基础，所以每个行业都将被改变。

### 面对 AI 的恐惧与责任

> For many, AI creates uncertainty. People see AI writing software, generating images, driving cars, and naturally wonder what happens next. Will jobs disappear? Will people be left behind? Will this technology become too powerful?
>
> 对许多人来说，AI 带来不确定性。人们看到 AI 写代码、生成图像、驾驶汽车，自然会问：接下来会发生什么？工作会消失吗？人们会被抛弃吗？这项技术会变得过于强大吗？

> Every major technological revolution in history created fear alongside opportunity. When society engages technology openly, responsibly, and optimistically, we expand human potential far more than we diminish it.
>
> 历史上每一次重大技术革命都伴随着恐惧与机遇并存。当社会以开放、负责任和乐观的方式拥抱技术，我们扩大人类潜能的幅度远大于削弱它。

> **Artificial intelligence, the automation of understanding, reasoning, and problem solving, is one of the most powerful technologies humanity has ever created. And like every transformative technology before it, it will bring both great promise and real risks.**
>
> **人工智能——理解、推理和问题解决的自动化——是人类有史以来创造的最强大的技术之一。和之前每一项变革性技术一样，它既带来巨大的希望，也带来真正的风险。**

> The responsibility of our generation is not only to advance AI, but to advance it wisely. Scientists and engineers have a profound responsibility to advance AI capabilities and AI safety together. Policymakers have a responsibility to create thoughtful guardrails that protect society, while still allowing innovation, discovery, and progress to move forward.
>
> 我们这代人的责任不仅是推进 AI，而是明智地推进 AI。科学家和工程师有责任同时推进 AI 能力和 AI 安全。政策制定者有责任制定深思熟虑的护栏来保护社会，同时仍允许创新、发现和进步向前发展。

> **History shows that societies that retreat from technology do not stop progress. They only surrender the opportunity to shape it and to benefit from it.**
>
> **历史表明，退缩于技术之外的社会并不能阻止进步——它们只是放弃了塑造进步并从中受益的机会。**

> **So, the answer is not to fear the future. The answer is to guide it wisely, build it responsibly, and ensure that its benefits reach as many people as possible. We should not teach fear of the future. We should engage it with optimism, responsibility, and ambition.**
>
> **所以，答案不是恐惧未来，而是明智地引导它、负责任地构建它，并确保它的益处惠及尽可能多的人。我们不应该教人恐惧未来，而应该以乐观、责任和雄心去参与未来。**

### AI 普惠化与基础设施

> Only a fraction of the people in the world know how to write software. Now, anyone can ask AI to build something useful. A shopkeeper can create a website and grow a business. A carpenter can design a kitchen and offer new services to clients. The AI writes the code. **Everyone is now a programmer.**
>
> 世界上只有一小部分人知道如何编写软件。现在，任何人都可以请 AI 构建有用的东西。小店老板可以建网站、发展业务；木匠可以设计厨房、向客户提供新服务。AI 来写代码。**每个人现在都是程序员。**

> For the first time, the power of computing and intelligence can truly reach everyone and close the technology divide.
>
> 有史以来第一次，计算和智能的力量能够真正触达每个人，弥合技术鸿沟。

> And like electricity and the internet before it, AI will require trillions of dollars of infrastructure investment. This is the largest technology infrastructure build-out in human history. And a once-in-a-generation opportunity to re-industrialize America and restore the nation's capacity to build.
>
> 和之前的电力和互联网一样，AI 将需要数万亿美元的基础设施投资。这是人类历史上最大的技术基础设施建设，也是一代人才有一次的机会：重新工业化美国，恢复这个国家的建造能力。

> Electricians, plumbers, iron workers, technicians, builders, this is your time. AI is not just creating a new computing industry, it is creating a new industrial era.
>
> 电工、水管工、钢铁工人、技术人员、建筑工人——这是属于你们的时代。AI 不仅在创造一个新的计算产业，它在创造一个全新的工业时代。

### AI 与工作：取代还是增强？

> AI will change every job. But the task and the purpose of a job are not the same. Many tasks will be automated. Some jobs will disappear. But many new jobs and entire new industries will be created.
>
> AI 将改变每一份工作。但工作的任务和工作的目的不是一回事。许多任务将被自动化。有些工作会消失。但许多新工作和全新的行业将被创造出来。

> **AI does not replace human purpose. It amplifies human capability.**
>
> **AI 不会取代人的目的，它放大人的能力。**

> **AI is not likely to replace you. But someone using AI better than you might.**
>
> **AI 不太可能取代你，但一个比你更会使用 AI 的人可能会。**

> Do we want our children to be supercharged by AI or be left behind by those who are?
>
> 我们想让孩子被 AI 超级赋能，还是被那些被 AI 超级赋能的人甩在后面？

### 结语：四大行动与 CMU 精神

> **We can and must do four things at once: Advance safely, create thoughtful policies, make AI broadly accessible, and encourage everyone to engage.** Everyone should have AI. Opportunities should not belong only to the people who can code.
>
> **我们能够也必须同时做到四件事：安全推进 AI、制定深思熟虑的政策、让 AI 广泛可及、鼓励人人参与。** 每个人都应该拥有 AI，机会不应该只属于会写代码的人。

> No generation has entered the world with more powerful tools or greater opportunities than you. We are all standing at the same starting line. This is your moment to help shape what comes next. **So run, don't walk.**
>
> 没有哪一代人踏入世界时拥有比你们更强大的工具、更巨大的机会。我们都站在同一条起跑线上。这是你们帮助塑造未来的时刻。**所以跑起来，别走。**

> **Carnegie Mellon has a motto I love: My heart is in the work.**
>
> **CMU 有一句我很爱的校训：我的心在事业中。**

> **So put your heart in the work. Build something worthy of your education, your potential, and the people who believed in you long before the world did.**
>
> **所以，把心投入你的工作。打造配得上你的教育、你的潜能，以及在全世界相信你之前老早就相信你的那些人的东西。**

> Congratulations, Carnegie Mellon class of 2026.
>
> 祝贺你们，卡内基梅隆大学 2026 届毕业生。
